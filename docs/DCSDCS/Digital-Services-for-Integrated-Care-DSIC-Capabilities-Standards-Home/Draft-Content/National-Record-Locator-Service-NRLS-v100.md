# Introduction

The National Record Locator Service enables authorised Health or Care Professionals to find and access Patient information shared by other Health or Care Organisations, to support the direct care of a Patient.

See [National Record Locator Service (NRLS) - NHS England Digital](https://digital.nhs.uk/services/national-record-locator) for further information.
