# Description {#EmergencyAlerts-Description}

Enables Health or Care Professionals to send and view Emergency Alerts that indicate an emergency or request for assistance.

# Outcomes {#EmergencyAlerts-Outcomes}

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| For a Health or Care Professional: | Supports the safety and wellbeing of Health or Care Professionals by sending Emergency Alerts to recipients that can be viewed and appropriate action taken. |
| For a Patient/Service User: | In the event of a medical emergency, a Health or Care Professional can send Emergency Alerts to call for assistance. |

| E00682 - send an Emergency Alert {#EmergencyAlerts-E00682-sendanEmergencyAlert} As a Health or Care Professional  I want to send an Emergency Alert to indicate an emergency or that assistance is required  So that I can signal an emergency is taking place or assistance is required Acceptance criterion 1: send an Emergency Alert {#EmergencyAlerts-Acceptancecriterion1:sendanEmergencyAlert} Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to send Emergency Alerts  When the Heath or Care Professional selects to send an Emergency Alert  Then the Emergency Alert is sent  And all specified recipients are notified E00682 - Additional Implementation Details {#EmergencyAlerts-E00682-AdditionalImplementationDetails} Solutions MUST comply with the following when implementing this Epic:   - The triggering of an Emergency Alert will not be obvious to the subject of the Alert - The Emergency Alert can be sent to multiple recipients E00682 - Supporting Information {#EmergencyAlerts-E00682-SupportingInformation}  - Devices capable of sending Emergency Alerts MAY include, but are not limited to:    - A computer desktop or laptop   - A standalone device (e.g. physical button)   - A handheld or wearable device   - A mobile phone - Devices MAY send Emergency Alerts without requiring Internet availability, GP Practice Solution availability, or for a Health or Care Professional to be logged in to use - Examples of recipients MAY include, but are not limited to:    - Health or Care Professionals   - External services (e.g. Emergency services)  ---  E00683 - view and acknowledge an Emergency Alert {#EmergencyAlerts-E00683-viewandacknowledgeanEmergencyAlert} As a recipient  I want to view an Emergency Alert that indicates an emergency or that assistance is required  So that appropriate action to resolve the Emergency Alert can be taken Acceptance criterion 1: view an Emergency Alert {#EmergencyAlerts-Acceptancecriterion1:viewanEmergencyAlert} Given the recipient is permitted to view Emergency Alerts  When an Emergency Alert is triggered  Then the Emergency Alert is displayed to all recipients Acceptance Criterion 2: acknowledge an Emergency Alert {#EmergencyAlerts-AcceptanceCriterion2:acknowledgeanEmergencyAlert} Given the recipient is permitted to acknowledge Emergency Alerts  When a recipient selects to acknowledge an Emergency Alert  Then the Emergency Alert is acknowledged  And all recipients are notified of the acknowledgement E00683 - Additional Implementation Details {#EmergencyAlerts-E00683-AdditionalImplementationDetails} Solutions MUST comply with the following when implementing this Epic:   - The Emergency Alert to include the location and name of the triggering Health or Care Professional - If covering multiple locations, the Emergency Alert to also include the Health or Care Organisation location details - The notification of acknowledgement of an Emergency Alert will not be obvious to the subject of the Alert E00683 - Supporting Information {#EmergencyAlerts-E00683-SupportingInformation}  - Health or Care Professionals MAY view or acknowledge an Emergency Alert without being logged in - Emergency Alerts MAY support multiple acknowledgements or remain visible after acknowledgement |

Additional Implementation Details {#EmergencyAlerts-E00686-AdditionalImplementationDetails} Solutions MUST comply with the following when implementing this Epic:   - If covering multiple locations, the evacuation list can be created per location  ---  E00687 - print evacuation list {#EmergencyAlerts-E00687-printevacuationlist} As a Health or Care Professional  I want to print evacuation lists for a Health or Care Organisation  So that attendance can be assessed following an Emergency Alert to evacuate a Health or Care Organisation Acceptance Criterion 1: print evacuation list {#EmergencyAlerts-AcceptanceCriterion1:printevacuationlist} Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Health or Care Organisation evacuation lists  When the Heath or Care Professional selects to print an evacuation list for a location  Then the evacuation list is printed   ---  E00688 - share evacuation list {#EmergencyAlerts-E00688-shareevacuationlist} As a Health or Care Professional  I want to share evacuation lists for a Health or Care Organisation  So that attendance can be assessed following an Emergency Alert to evacuate a Health or Care Organisation Acceptance Criterion 1: share evacuation list {#EmergencyAlerts-AcceptanceCriterion1:shareevacuationlist} Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Health or Care Organisation evacuation lists  When the Heath or Care Professional selects to share a Health or Care Organisation evacuation list  Then the evacuation list is shared E00688 - Supporting Information {#EmergencyAlerts-E00689-SupportingInformation}  - Examples of reports MAY include but are not limited to:    - Report on number of instances where an Emergency Alert was triggered   - Report on recipients acknowledging receiving notification of an Emergency Alert   - Report on the location within the Health or Care Organisation where Emergency Alerts have been triggered   - Report on the role of the Health or Care Professionals that have triggered Emergency Alerts   - Report on the number of test Emergency Alerts that have been triggered |
