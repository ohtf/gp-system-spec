# Description {#MedicalReports-Description}

Enables Health and Care Professionals to create, view and share Medical Reports for Patients.

# Outcomes {#MedicalReports-Outcomes}

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| For a Patient: | The ability for appropriate information from their Electronic Patient Record (EPR) to be shared with relevant 3rd parties. |
| For a Health or Care Professional: | The ability to create, view and share Medical Reports for Patients. |
