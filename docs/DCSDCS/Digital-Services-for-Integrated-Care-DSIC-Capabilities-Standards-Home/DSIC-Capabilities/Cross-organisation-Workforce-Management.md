# Description

Enables the creation of Service Schedules based on the requirements defined for services and on the resources available across a collection of Health or Care Organisations.

# Outcomes

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| For a Patient/Service User: | Maximises the availability of resources to best meet the health or care needs of the population. |
| For a Health or Care Professional: | Able to centrally manage the availability, scheduling and allocation of service resources (e.g. staff, equipment, rooms) across a number of organisations. |
| For a Health or Care Organisation: | Gain efficiencies across a number of organisations by minimising the number of empty rooms across the organisations, minimising travel for staff and maximising the availability or use of specific equipment. |
