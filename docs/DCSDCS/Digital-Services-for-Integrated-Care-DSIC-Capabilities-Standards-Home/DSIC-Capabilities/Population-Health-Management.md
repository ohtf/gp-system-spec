# Description

Supports Health or Care Professionals in accumulating and accessing health or care data from various sources, reporting on and creating Patients/Service User cohorts.

# Outcomes

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| For a Patient/Service User: | Receiving targeted interventions, treatment or care as a result of a health or care need being identified through analysis. |
| For Health or Care Organisations: | Enables models of care to focus on the local population's health or care needs by identifying and analysing Patient/Service User cohorts. |
| For the wider NHS and social care system: | Plan interventions, treatments and campaigns across the local population. |
