# Description {#AdviceandGuidance(ProfessionaltoProfessional)-Description}

Enables Health or Care Professionals to request and receive support from other Health or Care Professionals.

# Outcomes {#AdviceandGuidance(ProfessionaltoProfessional)-Outcomes}

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| For a Patient/Service User: | Experience more timely and informed decisions as the Health or Care Professionals involved in their care have access to specialist support. |
| For a Health or Care Professional: | Able to request and receive specialist support from other Health or Care Professionals. |
| For the wider NHS and social care system: | Able to drive collaborative working practices. |
