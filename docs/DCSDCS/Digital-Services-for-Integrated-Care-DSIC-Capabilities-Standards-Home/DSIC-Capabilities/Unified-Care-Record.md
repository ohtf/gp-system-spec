# Description

Supports Patient/Service User health and care information being brought together in a single consolidated record.

# Outcomes

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| For a Patient/Service User: | Removes the need to repeat their previously reported health and care information when they are seen by Health or Care Professionals and receive improved care because the Health or Care Professionals treating them are fully informed. |
| For a Health or Care Professional: | View a complete and up to date Unified Care Record for a Patient/Service User with health and care information gathered from different systems from a variety of health and care settings into a single record. Enables them to assess and treat the Patient/Service User more safely and effectively and can enable more integrated care across organisational boundaries through access to a common and shared view of the Patient/Service User's health and care information. |
