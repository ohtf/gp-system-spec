# Description {#OnlinePatientConsultation-Description}

Enables Patients/Patient Representatives to request and receive support relating to healthcare concerns online.

# Outcomes {#OnlinePatientConsultation-Outcomes}

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| For a Patient: | Able to have their consultations conducted online where appropriate, enabling appropriate prioritisation and direction to the most appropriate care. |
| For a Patient Representative: | Able to have a consultation conducted online on behalf of a Patient where appropriate, enabling appropriate prioritisation and direction to the most appropriate care. |
| For Primary Care: | Able to assign priority and most appropriate care to Online Patient Consultations through information provided by them, reducing the need for Patients to attend the Healthcare Organisation in person or be available at specific times. |
| For the wider NHS and social care system: | Improve Patient access to advice or consultation by providing an additional mode of access alongside traditional methods such as the telephone and walk ins. |
