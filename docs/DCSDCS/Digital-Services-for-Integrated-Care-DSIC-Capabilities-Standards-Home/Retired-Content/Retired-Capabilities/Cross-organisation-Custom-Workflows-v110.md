# Description {#Cross-organisationCustomWorkflowsv1.1.0-Description}

Supports the creation of custom Workflows for the management of activities across multiple Health or Care Organisations.

# Outcomes {#Cross-organisationCustomWorkflowsv1.1.0-Outcomes}

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| For a Patient/Service User: | Receive efficient services from Health or Care Organisations due to improved monitoring and management of cross-organisation activities related to their health and care. |
| For a Health or Care Organisations: | Supporting day-to-day activities across Health or Care Organisations with the ability to create and manage custom Workflows that support multi-disciplinary and multi-organisational teams. |
