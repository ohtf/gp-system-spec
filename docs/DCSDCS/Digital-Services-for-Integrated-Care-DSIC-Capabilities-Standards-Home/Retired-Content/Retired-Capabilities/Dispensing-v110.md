# Description

Supports the timely and effective dispensing of medicinal products and appliances to Patients, allowing Health or Care Professionals to dispense medication.

# Outcomes

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| For a Patient: | Prescriptions can be sent to Dispensaries electronically to manage dispensing requests more efficiently and reduce waiting times for Patients to access the right medicinal products and appliances. |
| For Primary Care: | Support the dispensing of medicinal products and appliances for Patients, and reduces the use of paper Prescriptions and provides a more efficient service to Patients. |
| For the wider NHS and social care system: | Support the dispensing of medicinal products and appliances. |
