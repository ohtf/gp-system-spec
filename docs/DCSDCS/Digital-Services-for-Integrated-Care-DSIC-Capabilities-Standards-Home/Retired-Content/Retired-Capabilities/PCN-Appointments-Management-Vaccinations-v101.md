# Description

The PCN Appointments Management - Vaccinations Capability enables the administration and scheduling of vaccination appointments for Patients.

The Capability may also enable:

- the sending of vaccination invite communications and booking reminders to prompt Patients to book vaccination appointments
- the sending of vaccination appointment notification and reminders
- the ability for Patients to manage their own vaccination appointments

# Outcomes

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Patient: | - Ability to have vaccination appointments booked, so they can receive vaccinations safely and efficiently as part of the PCN led Vaccination Programme |
| Healthcare Organisation: | - Ability to manage vaccination appointment availability for vaccination sites to support the PCN led Vaccination Programme - Ability to manage vaccination appointments for Patients registered across different GP Practices |
