# Description {#ViewRecord-Citizenv1.0.3-Description}

Enables Citizens to view content from their Electronic Patient Record (EPR) online. Integrates with Patient Information Maintenance - GP Solution.

# Outcomes {#ViewRecord-Citizenv1.0.3-Outcomes}

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| For a Citizen: | Having remote access to content from their Electronic Patient Record (EPR), reducing the need to contact the Healthcare Organisation to view information resulting in time saved, and enabling self-care. |
| For General Practice: | Maintenance of an online health record supports the remote care of Patients and maintaining positive relationships. It saves time for staff by reducing the need for Patients to contact the Healthcare Organisation (e.g. no need for Patients to call for test results and Patients are more prepared before their Appointment) and supports effective communication with Patients. |
