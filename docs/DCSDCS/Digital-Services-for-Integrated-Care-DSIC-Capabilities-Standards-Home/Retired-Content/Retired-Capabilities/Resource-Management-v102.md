# Description

Supports the management of Health or Care Organisation site information including management of Staff Members at Health or Care Organisations.

# Outcomes

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| For Health or Care Organisation: | Information about Staff Members working for the Health or Care Organisation is easier to manage. |
| For the wider NHS and social care system: | Efficient and appropriate management of Health or Care Organisation Staff Members to improve services and health outcomes for Patient/Service User. |
