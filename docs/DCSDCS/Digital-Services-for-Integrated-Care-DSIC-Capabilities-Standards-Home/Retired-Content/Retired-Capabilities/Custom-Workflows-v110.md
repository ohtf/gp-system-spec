# Description

Supports the creation of custom Workflows for the management of activities within the Health or Care Organisation.

# Outcomes

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| For a Patient/Service User: | Receiving efficient service as the Health or Care Organisation is easily able to monitor and manage activities related to their health and care. |
| For a Health or Care Organisation: | Supporting day-to-day activities in a Health or Care Organisation with the ability to create and manage custom Workflows. |
| For the wider NHS and social care system: | Supporting and facilitating the coordination of Patient or Service User care across the health and social care system. |
