# Requirements

| Epic Mapping | Requirement ID | Requirement Text | Level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C1E1 | **GP-SPFS-5.2.2-01A** | **Appointment Management - View and Book - Patient**  Allow Patients to view, book (available slots as defined by the Practice) and cancel an Appointment.   - Patients are able to view all past and future Appointments. - Appointment Booking is prevented when the maximum number of future Appointments is reached. The maximum is set by the Practice. - Appointment Bookings will not require Practice Staff intervention unless there are constraints or technical issues.   See E00221 in the Appointments Management - GP Capability for defining available slots | MUST |
| C1E2 | **AMC01** | **Appointment Management - View and Book - Proxy**  Allow Proxies to view, book (available slots as defined by the Practice) and cancel an Appointment.   - Proxies are able to view all past and future Appointments. - Appointment Booking is prevented when the maximum number of future Appointments is reached. The maximum is set by the Practice. - Appointment Bookings will not require Practice Staff intervention unless there are constraints or technical issues.   See E00221 in the Appointments Management - GP Capability for defining available slots | MAY |
| C1E1 | **GP-SPFS-5.2.2-01B** | **Appointment Management - Free Text - Patient**  Allow Patients to optionally add 'free text' to accompany the Appointment. Also display an appropriate clinical warning, including a disclaimer/message that 'free text' can be seen by non-clinical staff and not read until the time of the Appointment. | MUST |
| C1E2 | **AMC02** | **Appointment Management - Free Text - Proxy**  Allow Proxies to optionally add 'free text' to accompany the Appointment. Also display an appropriate clinical warning, including a disclaimer/message that 'free text' can be seen by non-clinical staff and not read until the time of the Appointment. | MAY |
| C1E1 | **GP-PPFS-3.2-04** | **Transactional Services - Appointments Service**  Support Appointment management rules and restrictions via the management of Sessions and individual Appointment Slots (see E00221 in the Appointments Management - GP Capability), in addition:   - Preventing duplicate Appointment Booking | MUST |
| C1E1 | **GP-PPFS-3.2-05A** | **Transactional Services - Appointments for Citizens with an OSA**  Support Appointments that can be made available, and then used by Citizen Service Users (see E00221 in the Appointments Management - GP Capability). Citizens with an Online Service Account (OSA) will only be able to view or cancel Appointments that they themselves have booked online.  Citizens with an OSA are able to book one Appointment to complete their registration for Citizen Services. | MUST |
