# Requirements

| Epic Mapping | Requirement ID | Requirement Text | Level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Epic 1 | **GP-SPFS-5.2.3-01** | **Communication Management - Patient**  Allow the Patient to create, receive, view, and respond to communications with the Patient's Practice.  See GP-PPFS-3.2-07 for incorporation of communications into Patient Record. | MUST |
| Epic 2 | **GP-CWP-4** | **Communication Management - Proxy**  Allow the Proxy to create, receive, view, and respond to communications with the Patient's Practice.  See GP-PPFS-3.2-07 for incorporation of communications into Patient Record. | MAY |
| Epic 1 | **GP-CWP-1** | **Communication Attachments - Patient**  Allow Patients to attach documents to their communications. | MUST |
| Epic 2 | **GP-CWP-5** | **Communication Attachments - Proxy**  Allow Proxies to attach documents to their communications. | MAY |
| Epic 1 | **GP-CWP-2** | **Organise Messages - Patient**  Allow Patients to organise their messages. | MAY |
| Epic 2 | **GP-CWP-6** | **Organise Messages - Proxy**  Allow Proxies to organise their messages. | MAY |
| Epic 1 | **GP-PPFS-6.2-05** | **Patient Notification of Patient-Practice Communications**  Notify a Patient via their preferred Verified Contact Details (see COM1) that they have received a message from the Practice. When the message is related to an existing communication (e.g. a reply to an existing message), only Patients involved in the communication will need to be notified. | MUST |
| Epic 2 | **GP-CWP-7** | **Proxy Notification of Patient-Practice Communications**  Notify a Proxy via their preferred Verified Contact Details (see COM1) that they or the Patient have received a message from the Practice. When the message is related to an existing communication (e.g. a reply to an existing message), only Patients and Proxies involved in the communication will need to be notified. | MAY |
| Epic 1 | **GP-PPFS-6.3-07** | **Practice Notification (Task) of Patient-Practice Communications**  Notify Practice Users e.g. by generating a notification, that a message has been received, whether Citizen initiated or in response to previous communications.  Such notification to be assigned to a specified Practice User, Role or Group as per Practice Configuration.  See GP-PPFS-3.3-06 for configuring Patient-Practice communications  See Workflow - Standard | MUST |
| Epic 1 | **GP-CWP-3** | **Display Configurable Disclaimer - Patient**  A practice configurable disclaimer to be displayed to Patients when creating a communication so that important information about this service is always visible.  See GP-PPFS-3.3-14 for practice configuration of disclaimer. | MUST |
| Epic 2 | **GP-CWP-8** | **Display Configurable Disclaimer - Proxy**  A practice configurable disclaimer to be displayed to Proxies when creating a communication so that important information about this service is always visible.  See GP-PPFS-3.3-14 for practice configuration of disclaimer. | MAY |

All supplier Solutions will need to meet this Standard if they are delivering the Communicate With Practice - Citizen Capability
