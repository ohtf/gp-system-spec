# Requirements

| Epic Mapping | Requirement ID | Requirement Text | Level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C6E1, C6E4 | GP-05.5-01 | **Access to decision support resources**  Provide access to multiple decision support resources. For example, from:   - Within the Patient Record - Relevant Solutions (such as Prescribing and Recording Consultations - GP)   This includes support for the implementation and application of both national and local guidelines. | MUST |
| C6E2 | GP-DS-6.1-03 | **CDS Triggering - Local Configuration**  CDS triggering to be configurable at Practice level, including:   - Ability to configure by Staff Member - Ability to configure by Staff Role - Ability to enable/disable triggering of individual tools | MUST |
| C6E3 | GP-DS-2.5-4 | **Monitor Effectiveness of the Decision Support Service**  Capability to undertake analysis of the usage and effectiveness of the service across groups of GP Practices e.g. at CCG level | MUST |
