# Digital Pathways Solution Registry (Market Engagement) Home

### Welcome to the Digital Pathways Solution Registry Capabilities and Standards site {#DigitalPathwaysSolutionRegistry(MarketEngagement)Home-WelcometotheDigitalPathwaysSolutionRegistryCapabilitiesandStandardssite}

The Digital Pathways Solution Registry is a list of Suppliers who have self-declared their Solution complies with the Digital Pathways Solution Registry Capabilities and Standards. The Digital Pathways Solution Registry is always live, and the purpose is to inform and support buyers to use the Supplier self-declared information held in the Registry to further develop their strategy to buy.

Buyers will need to undertake their own due diligence when contracting. The Digital Pathways Solution Registry acts as a repository of learning for buyers, aligned with the requirements set out in the [**GP IT Operating Model**](https://www.england.nhs.uk/digitaltechnology/digital-primary-care/gp-digital-services-operating-model/). Suppliers must engage with the National Commercial and Procurement Hub to support the buying journey.

The Digital Pathways Solution Registry includes Capabilities, Overarching Standards and other applicable Standards as defined by individual Epics.

The DSIC [**Baseline Assurance Standard**](../../DCSDCS/Digital-Services-for-Integrated-Care-DSIC-Capabilities-Standards-Home/DSIC-Standards/Overarching-Standards/Baseline-Assurance-Standard.md) is being used to set minimum set of essential Requirements from the DSIC Overarching Standards for the Digital Pathways Registry. All Suppliers should disregard references to the Buying Catalogue and the Solution Category A, B, C columns, and should self certify against all requirements with evidence.

View the [**Digital Services for Integrated Care (DSIC) Roadmap**](DSIC-Roadmap.md) for information about all future changes to DSIC Capabilities and Standards.

Click on relevant Capability or Standard below to view further information. If you are new to this site, please view the [**Introduction to Capabilities and Standards**](Introduction-to-Capabilities-and-Standards/index.md) and [**Capability and Standards Structure**](Introduction-to-Capabilities-and-Standards/Capability-and-Standards-Structure.md) pages.

To view a short description for a Capability or Standard, click 'More'.

View [**Capabilities with Interoperability Relationships**](Capabilities-with-Interoperability-Relationships.md) to understand which interoperability requirements are associated with each Capability.

View the [**Master Glossary**](Master-Glossary.md) for definitions of key terms.

For guidance on the current best practices throughout the IT industry when provisioning, deploying and operating infrastructure and services, please view the [**Recommended Best Practices**](Recommended-Best-Practices.md) page.
