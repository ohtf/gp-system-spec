# Capability and Standards Overview

**A buyer introduction to capabilities and standards**

**Preliminaries**

The terminology used below assumes that the reader has familiarised themselves with some key Digital Care Services concepts available via the following guidance / tutorials:

- [GPITF Overview](https://nhse-dsic.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/BG/overview)

**Guidance**

**What is a capability?**

Within GP IT Futures functional requirements are expressed at the highest level as [capabilities](https://gpitbjss.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GPITF/overview) (e.g. [Recording Consultations](../../../DCSDCS/Digital-Services-for-Integrated-Care-DSIC-Capabilities-Standards-Home/DSIC-Capabilities/Consultation-Management-GP.md)). Each [capability](https://gpitbjss.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GPITF/overview) includes the following:

Description: A brief description of the [capability's](https://gpitbjss.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GPITF/overview)scope.

Outcomes: The key business outcomes encompassed by the [capability](https://gpitbjss.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GPITF/overview).

Epics: A [capability](https://gpitbjss.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GPITF/overview) comprises one or more epics (i.e. user stories). Each epic has a brief description and one or more acceptance criteria (i.e. the criteria that must be met in order to evidence that a C[atalogue Solution](http://buyingcatalogue.digital.nhs.uk) provides the function(s) encompassed by the epic.

The use of [capabilities](https://gpitbjss.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GPITF/overview) is intended to allow suppliers to innovate due to the less prescriptive nature of the requirements expression and to segment the requirements in a way that is meaningful for buyers.

An example of the [Record Consultations](../../../DCSDCS/Digital-Services-for-Integrated-Care-DSIC-Capabilities-Standards-Home/DSIC-Capabilities/Consultation-Management-GP.md) capability description, outcomes and an epic are provided below to illustrate.

# **Capability description** {#CapabilityandStandardsOverview-Capabilitydescription}

Supports the standardised recording of Consultations and other General Practice activities. Also supports the extraction of Female Genital Mutilation (FGM) data for the FGM data set.

# **Capability outcomes** {#CapabilityandStandardsOverview-Capabilityoutcomes}

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| For a Patient: | There is a detailed and standardised history of consultations including those between Healthcare Professionals that supports more efficient and timely diagnosis and treatment. |
| For General Practice: | Standardised recording and maintenance of accurate Consultation data. Enable timely and efficient access to Consultation data to support Patient care and safety. |
| For the wider NHS and social care system: | Enabling Healthcare Professionals from a variety of services and settings to access accurate and detailed Consultation data in support of decision making and provision of Patient services. |

### **Epic description: C15E1 - record Consultation information** {#CapabilityandStandardsOverview-Epicdescription:C15E1-recordConsultationinformation}

As a Practice User

I want to record the information associated with a Consultation

So that the Consultation is recorded

### **Epic Acceptance criterion 1: record Consultation information** {#CapabilityandStandardsOverview-EpicAcceptancecriterion1:recordConsultationinformation}

Given the Practice User is permitted to record Consultations

When the Practice User chooses to record information associated with a Consultation

Then the Consultation is recorded.

The following capabilities are referred to as foundation capabilities and are important to understand as they represent a gateway into the receipt of GMS funding

- Appointments management - GP
- GP referral management
- GP resource management
- Patient information maintenance
- Prescribing
- Recording consultations.

In addition to the predefined [capabilities,](https://gpitbjss.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GPITF/overview) suppliers can define [Productivity Capabilities](The-Productivity-Capability.md) for functions that are applicable to primary care, but are not encompassed by the predefined [capabilities](https://gpitbjss.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GPITF/overview). These [productivity capabilities](The-Productivity-Capability.md) will be grouped into themes and the [Catalogue Solutions](http://buyingcatalogue.digital.nhs.uk) mapping to them will be available via the [Catalogue](http://buyingcatalogue.digital.nhs.uk).

**What are capabilities used for?**

Capabilities will be used by:

- NHS Digital to express the business functions within the scope of GP IT Futures
- Suppliers to map their [Catalogue Solutions](http://buyingcatalogue.digital.nhs.uk) to the capabilities and epics within those capabilities i.e. it allows suppliers to describe the functional scope of their solutions using a standardised language
- NHS Digital to assure [catalogue solutions](http://buyingcatalogue.digital.nhs.uk) – see here for more detail
- Suppliers to understand the scope of solutions that may be offered via GP IT Futures and to see market opportunities
- Buyers to search for [Catalogue Solutions](http://buyingcatalogue.digital.nhs.uk) that meet their needs on the [Buying Catalogue](http://buyingcatalogue.digital.nhs.uk), where the needs are expressed using the capabilities / epics.

**What are Standards?**

Standards describe the technical or operating conditions applicable to [Catalogue Solutions.](http://buyingcatalogue.digital.nhs.uk) The following different classifications of [Standard](https://gpitbjss.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GPITF/overview) are used in GP IT Futures:

[Overarching Standards:](../../../DCSDCS/Digital-Services-for-Integrated-Care-DSIC-Capabilities-Standards-Home/DSIC-Standards/Overarching-Standards/index.md) These apply to all c[apabilities](https://gpitbjss.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GPITF/overview) and therefore all solutions (e.g. the [Clinical Safety Standard](../../../DCSDCS/Digital-Services-for-Integrated-Care-DSIC-Capabilities-Standards-Home/DSIC-Standards/Overarching-Standards/Clinical-Safety.md)).

[Context Specific Standards:](../../../DCSDCS/Context-Specific-Standards.md) These apply to one or more specific [capabilities](https://gpitbjss.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GPITF/overview) (e.g. the [Interoperability Standard](../../../DCSDCS/Digital-Services-for-Integrated-Care-DSIC-Capabilities-Standards-Home/DSIC-Standards/Overarching-Standards/Interoperability-Standard.md)).

Capability Specific Standards: Many [capabilities](https://gpitbjss.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GPITF/overview) have one of these [standards.](https://gpitbjss.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GPITF/overview) These [standards](https://gpitbjss.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GPITF/overview) express additional functional requirements for the [capability.](https://gpitbjss.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/GPITF/overview)

**What are Standards used for?**

Standards will be used by:

- NHS Digital to express the technical or operating conditions applicable to [capabilities](../../../GPITF/GP-IT-Futures-Capabilities-and-Standards-Home/Introduction-to-Capabilities-and-Standards/index.md) and to drive the technical maturity and interoperability objectives of GP IT Futures
- NHS Digital to assure [catalogue solutions](http://buyingcatalogue.digital.nhs.uk) meet the applicable [Standards](../../../GPITF/GP-IT-Futures-Capabilities-and-Standards-Home/Introduction-to-Capabilities-and-Standards/index.md) – see here for more detail
