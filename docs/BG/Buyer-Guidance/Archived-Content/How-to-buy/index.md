# Introduction

The purpose of these confluence pages is to help you understand how to use the GP IT Futures Digital Care Services Framework to procure [Catalogue Solutions](http://buyingcatalogue.digital.nhs.uk) or to vary your existing Call Off Agreements under common business change scenarios.

The common purchasing and business change scenarios of relevance to GPITF are set out in [Common purchasing and business scenarios](../Common-purchasing-and-business-change-scenarios.md)

For many of the [purchasing scenarios](../Common-purchasing-and-business-change-scenarios.md) you will need to:

**Understand your requirement:** The scope of [Catalogue Solutions](http://buyingcatalogue.digital.nhs.uk) is expressed in terms of the Capabilities and Epics to which they map. You should therefore determine the Capabilities and Epics you require before commencing your procurement.

[**Understand Pricing**](Executing-a-procurement-a-step-by-step-guide/Understanding-Price.md)**:** There are a range of pricing models referred to as units of order applicable to [Catalogue Solutions](http://buyingcatalogue.digital.nhs.uk). The Catalogue should explain those applicable to each [Catalogue Solution](http://buyingcatalogue.digital.nhs.uk). However, further detail on this topic is set out in [Understanding Price](Executing-a-procurement-a-step-by-step-guide/Understanding-Price.md) should you need it.

**Selecting the most appropriate procurement procedure:** There are three procurement procedures available under the GP IT Futures Framework.  They are:

- [**Direct Award**](Executing-a-procurement-a-step-by-step-guide/Direct-Award.md) - low complexity contracts valued up to £40,000. The decision to execute a Direct Award for a [Catalogue solution](http://buyingcatalogue.digital.nhs.uk) can be determined using information on the [Buying Catalogue.](http://buyingcatalogue.digital.nhs.uk)
- [**Further Competition On-Catalogue**](Executing-a-procurement-a-step-by-step-guide/Further-Competition-On-Catalogue.md) - low complexity contracts valued up to £250,000. A Further Competition On-Catalogue procedure can be executed using information available on the [Buying Catalogue](http://buyingcatalogue.digital.nhs.uk) and requires an evaluation of [Catalogue solutions](http://buyingcatalogue.digital.nhs.uk) to determine suitability.
- [**Further Competition Off-Catalogue**](Executing-a-procurement-a-step-by-step-guide/Further-Competition-Off-Catalogue.md) - high complexity and high-value contracts. A Further Competition Off Catalogue procedure requires an Invitation to Tender issued to [Catalogue Solution](http://buyingcatalogue.digital.nhs.uk) suppliers, and an evaluation process informed by information on the [Buying Catalogue.](http://buyingcatalogue.digital.nhs.uk)

Once you have entered into a Call Off Agreement it is the Call Off Ordering Party’s (as specified on the Call Off Order Form) responsibility to manage that Call Off Agreement. A [contract overview](../Contractual-Overview.md) is provided to assist you with understanding the Call Off Agreements.
