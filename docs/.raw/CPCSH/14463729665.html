Welcome to the Community Pharmacy Clinical Services Capabilities and Standards site

The Community Pharmacy Clinical Services Contracting Vehicle supports the management of Referrals and the recording of Consultations for specific Clinical Services offered by Community Pharmacies.

This Contracting Vehicle is managed by a Supplementary Care Standard called Community Pharmacy Clinical Services. It includes Functional Requirements and Secondary Artefacts.

Suppliers must meet all MUST Requirements in the Community Pharmacy Clinical Services Supplementary Care Standard to onboard onto the NHS Buying Catalogue for this Contracting Vehicle. For further information on the onboarding process, see DSIC Supplier Onboarding Guidance.

Supplementary Care Standard

  • Community Pharmacy Clinical Services

More...

Supports Referrals to Community Pharmacy and the management of Community Pharmacy Consultations. Also supports the submission of data to the NHS Business Services Authority (NHSBSA) for payment purposes.

This Standard mandates the requirements for systems used to deliver the following Clinical Services:

  • Pharmacy First

    • Minor Illness

    • Urgent Medicines

    • Clinical Pathways

  • Pharmacy Contraception

  • Pharmacy Blood Pressure Check
