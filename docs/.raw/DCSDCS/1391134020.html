Description

Supports the creation of custom Workflows for the management of activities within the Health or Care Organisation.

Outcomes

For a Patient/Service User:

Receiving efficient service as the Health or Care Organisation is easily able to monitor and manage activities related to their health and care.

For a Health or Care Organisation:

Supporting day-to-day activities in a Health or Care Organisation with the ability to create and manage custom Workflows.

For the wider NHS and social care system:

Supporting and facilitating the coordination of Patient or Service User care across the health and social care system.

E00397 - manage custom Workflows

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to manage custom Workflows

So that I can manage Workflows that are customised for my Health or Care Organisation

Acceptance criterion 1: create a custom Workflow

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage custom Workflows

When the Health or Care Professional selects to create a custom Workflow

Then the custom Workflow is created

Acceptance criterion 2: view a custom Workflow 

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage custom Workflows

When the Health or Care Professional selects to view a custom Workflow 

Then the custom Workflow is displayed

Acceptance criterion 3: amend a custom Workflow

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage custom Workflows

When the Health or Care Professional selects to amend a custom Workflow

Then the custom Workflow is amended

Acceptance criterion 4: delete a custom Workflow

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage custom Workflows

When the Health or Care Professional selects to delete a custom Workflow

Then the custom Workflow is deleted

E00397 - Supporting Information

  • Workflow data items MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • Workflow type

    • Workflow description

    • Creator

    • Creation date and time

    • Status (e.g. To Do, In Progress or Complete)

    • Workflow Event(s), including:

      • Sequential

      • Parallel 

      • Conditional

      • Manual

      • Automated time based (e.g. automatic updating of event after a certain time period has passed)

      • Automated data driven (e.g. automatic triggering of event based on data being recorded)

    • Supporting information (e.g. a link to a document or URL to external website)

    • Expected duration (e.g. time from generation to expected completion)

    • Workflow assignee(s)

    • Priority

  • Examples of Workflow Events MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • A Task (see Task Management Capability)

    • A medication request

    • A document review
