Description

Enables Patients to make specific types of clinical/non clinical requests online to their Healthcare Organisation.

Outcomes

For a Patient:

Able to make clinical/non clinical requests to a Healthcare Organisation.

For a Patient Representative:

Able to make clinical/non clinical requests to a Healthcare Organisation on behalf of a Patient.

For Primary Care:

To help reduce the administrative burden on Healthcare professionals by freeing up time to focus on providing patient care.

For the wider NHS and social care system:

Improve efficiency and communication between Patient and Healthcare Organisation by providing an additional mode of communication alongside traditional methods such as the telephone and walk ins.

E00584 - Online Patient Request

As a Patient

I want to create an Online Patient Request

So that I can submit an Online Patient Request to a Healthcare Organisation

Acceptance criterion 1: create an Online Patient Request

Given the Patient has accessed the Online Patient Request Solution

When the Patient selects to create an Online Patient Request with the Healthcare Organisation

Then the Patient's demographic and contact details are recorded

And the Online Patient Request is created

E00584 - Additional Implementation Details

Solutions MUST comply with the following when implementing this Epic:

  • Online Patient Request to include the following demographic and contact details for the Patient:

    • Name

    • Date of birth

    • Sex

    • Address

    • Mobile/Telephone number (where available)

    • Email (where available)

    • NHS Number

E00584 - Supporting information

  • Examples of Online Requests MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • Request Repeat Prescription

    • Request test results

    • Request fit note

    • Submit a Health Review form

    • Request update to the Patient's demographic & contact details

    • Message Healthcare Organisation

E00789 - verify a Patient's demographic details recorded by a Patient Representative using the Personal Demographics Service (PDS) for an Online Patient Request

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to verify a Patient’s demographic details recorded by a Patient Representative are correct using the Personal Demographics Service (PDS)

So that the Patient’s demographic details are accurate

Acceptance Criterion 1: verify a Patient’s demographic details recorded by a Patient Representative using the Personal Demographics Service (PDS) for an Online Patient Request

Given the Patient Representative has accessed the Online Patient Request Solution

When the Patient Representative selects to create an Online Patient Request on behalf of a Patient

And the Patient Representative has recorded the Patient’s demographic details

Then the Patient’s demographic details are verified using the Personal Demographics Service (PDS)

E00789 - Additional Implementation Details

Solutions MUST comply with the following when implementing this Epic:

  • Personal Demographics Service (PDS) Standard

  • The results from the Personal Demographics Service (PDS) must NOT be shared with the Patient or Patient Representative

E00789 - Supporting Information

  • Verifying the Patient's demographic details supports Health of Care Professionals to identify Patients that are registered with the Practice from those that potentially might not be

  • Where a Patient's demographic details cannot be verified, Health of Care Professionals may wish to utilise functionality such as that described in E00794 - manage unmatched Online Patient Requests

E00794 - Supporting Information
