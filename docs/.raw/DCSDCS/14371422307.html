Description

Enables Citizens to view content from their Electronic Patient Record (EPR) online. Integrates with Patient Information Maintenance - GP Solution.

Outcomes

For a Citizen:

Having remote access to content from their Electronic Patient Record (EPR), reducing the need to contact the Healthcare Organisation to view information resulting in time saved, and enabling self-care.

For General Practice:

Maintenance of an online health record supports the remote care of Patients and maintaining positive relationships. It saves time for staff by reducing the need for Patients to contact the Healthcare Organisation (e.g. no need for Patients to call for test results and Patients are more prepared before their Appointment) and supports effective communication with Patients.

E00357 - Patient viewing Summary Information Record

As a Patient

I want to view my Summary Information Record

So that I can see a summary of the information from my Electronic Patient Record (EPR)

Acceptance criterion 1: view Summary Information Record

Given the Patient is enabled for the View Record Citizen Service

And the Patient is enabled for the Summary Information Record Access Level

When the Patient selects to view the Summary Information Record

Then the Summary Information Record is displayed

E00357 - Additional Implementation Details

Solutions MUST comply with the following when implementing this Epic:

  • Utilise Provider IM1 Patient interface(s) and adhere to IM1 - Interface Mechanism Standard (Patient interface Consumer requirements)

  • The Summary Information Record dataset to only include medications, allergies and adverse reactions – specific requirements for this dataset are as defined within the Summary Care Records requirements documentation

  • Only the Summary Information Record dataset will be made available irrespective of any agreement with the Patient regarding their actual Summary Care Record (i.e. if certain information for the Patient is excluded from their Summary Care Record, this information will not automatically be excluded from the Summary Information Record and vice versa)

E00357 - Supporting Information

  • For managing the View Record Citizen Service, see manage View Record Citizen Service Access for the Practice and manage View Record Citizen Service Access for a Patient in the Patient Information Maintenance - GP Capability

  • For information on elements of the Electronic Patient Record (EPR) marked as ‘Restricted from View’ seeconfigure access to elements of the Electronic Patient Record (EPR) in the View Record Citizen Service in the Patient Information Maintenance - GP Capability

  • Further information on Citizen Services can be found in the Citizen Services Implementation Guidance
