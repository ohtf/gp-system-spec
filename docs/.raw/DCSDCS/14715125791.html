Introduction

The Vaccinations Administration (DigiMeds) Standard supports updates to a Patient’s registered GP Practice about vaccinations administered at another Health or Care Organisation.

Requirements

Applicable Suppliers

ID

Requirement

Level

GP Suppliers

DMPF01

Implement and maintain the DigiMeds specification for Vaccination Event Data Flow inline with the latest specification version to receive updates of the administration of vaccinations to a Patient at another Health or Care Organisation.

The following vaccinations to be supported:

  • BCG

  • COVID-19

  • Hep B

  • Hib/MenC

  • Human Papillomavirus (HPV)

  • Influenza (Flu)

  • Measles, Mumps, Rubella (MMR)

  • Measles, Mumps, Rubella, and Varicella (MMRV)

  • MenACWY

  • MenB

  • Pertussis

  • Pneumococcal

  • Respiratory Syncytial Virus (RSV)

  • Rotavirus

  • Shingles

  • 3 in 1

  • 4 in 1 

  • 6 in 1
