Introduction

This page details the Schedule 3, 4 and 5 Controlled Drugs where validation on the Maximum Quantity is to be implemented to ensure the Quantity recorded does not exceed the Maximum Quantity for 5 days supply.

This list will be reviewed annually.

Suppliers will need to apply validation to the Quantity recorded of the listed dm+d Virtual Medicinal Products (VMPs) and any related Actual Medicinal Products (AMPs) or Actual Medicinal Product Packs (AMPPs) as per the latest dm+d update.
