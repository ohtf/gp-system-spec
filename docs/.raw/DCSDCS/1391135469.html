Description

Supports Health or Care Professionals in accumulating and accessing health or care data from various sources, reporting on and creating Patients/Service User cohorts.

Outcomes

For a Patient/Service User:

Receiving targeted interventions, treatment or care as a result of a health or care need being identified through analysis.

For Health or Care Organisations:

Enables models of care to focus on the local population's health or care needs by identifying and analysing Patient/Service User cohorts.

For the wider NHS and social care system:

Plan interventions, treatments and campaigns across the local population.

E00618 - report on Population Health Management data

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to report on Population Health Management data

So that I can analyse the data and make informed decisions

Acceptance criterion 1: define Population Health Management report criteria

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Population Health Management reports

When the Health or Care Professional selects to define the criteria for the report

Then the criteria for the report is defined

Acceptance criterion 2: view Population Health Management reports

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to view Population Health Management reports

When the Health or Care Professional selects to view the report

Then the report results are displayed
