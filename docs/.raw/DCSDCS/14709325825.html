Description

Supports the self-service Appointment check-in process for Patients.

Outcomes

For a Patient:

Enables the Patient to check in for their Appointment without the need to speak to a Health or Care Professional.

For Health or Care Organisations:

Enables Health or Care Organisations to optimise staff capacity by reducing the need for manual Patient check in.

MUST Epics - Describes the minimum functionality required to deliver a Capability. Catalogue Solutions MUST be successfully evaluated against each Epic and Acceptance Criteria via Capability Assessment in order to be associated with this Capability.

E00716 - Patient checks in for an Appointment using self-service

As a Patient

I want to use self-service to check in for my Appointment

So that I can check in for my Appointment without contacting a Health or Care Professional

Acceptance criterion 1: Patient checks in for an Appointment using self-service

Given the Patient self-service check-in for Appointments is enabled

When the Patient selects to check in for an Appointment

And the Patient is matched to an Appointment

Then the Patient is recorded as checked in

And the Patient is notified they have checked in

Acceptance criterion 2: Patient is not matched to an Appointment at self-service

Given the Patient self-service check-in for Appointments is enabled

When the Patient selects to check in for an Appointment

And the Patient is not matched to an Appointment

Then the Patient is not recorded as checked in

And the Patient is notified they have not checked in

E00716 - Additional Implementation Details

Solutions MUST comply with the following when implementing this Epic:

  • Solutions which integrate with the GP Core Clinical Solution to adhere to:

    • IM1 - Interface Mechanism Standard (Practice Consumer requirements)

E00716 - Supporting Information

  • Examples of methods of self-service Appointment check-in MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • Touch screen computer in the waiting room

    • Personal mobile device

E00721 - display pseudonym for checked in Patients in an Appointment queue

As a Patient

I want my pseudonym to be displayed in an Appointment queue

So that my personal identity remains confidential while still allowing me to recognise my place in the Appointment queue

Acceptance criterion 1: assign pseudonyms to Patients at self-service Appointment check-in

Given the Patient self-service check-in for Appointments is enabled

When the Patient selects to check in for an Appointment

And the Patient selects to assign themselves a pseudonym

Then the Patient is assigned a pseudonym

And the pseudonym is displayed to the Patient

And the Patient is recorded as checked in

Acceptance criterion 2: display Patient’s pseudonyms in an Appointment queue

Given the Patient has been assigned a pseudonym

When the Patient has checked in for an Appointment

Then the Patient’s pseudonym is displayed in an Appointment queue

And the Patient’s name is not displayed

E00721 - Supporting information

  • Examples of Appointment queue displays MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • Call board

    • Digital screen (e.g. a tablet)

    • Mobile phone

  • Examples of pseudonyms for Patients MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • A number

    • A partially redacted name
