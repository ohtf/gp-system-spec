GP-05.5-01

Access to decision support resources

Provide access to multiple decision support resources. For example, from:

  • Within the Patient Record
  • Relevant Solutions (such as Prescribing and Recording Consultations)

This includes support for the implementation and application of both national and local guidelines.

GP-DS-6.1-03

CDS Triggering - Local Configuration

CDS triggering to be configurable at Practice level, including:

  • Ability to configure by Staff Member
  • Ability to configure by Staff Role 
  • Ability to enable/disable triggering of individual tools
