Contracting Vehicle(s)

Community Pharmacy Clinical Services

Description

Enables Health or Care Professionals to record Community Pharmacy Consultation Information for Patients and submit this Consultation Information to the Patient’s registered GP Practice and the NHSBSA.

Outcomes

For a Patient:

There is a detailed and standardised history of consultations recorded and submitted to their registered GP Practice.

For Community Pharmacy Clinical Services:

Management of Consultations with technology provides better efficiency to the Organisation by using mechanisms to record and inform other Health or Care Organisations.

E00625 - manage Pharmacy First Consultation Information for Patients

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to manage Pharmacy First Consultation Information for Patients

So that Consultation Information is retained for Patients

Acceptance criterion 1: record Pharmacy First Minor Illness Consultation Information for Patients

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Consultation Information for Patients

When the Health or Care Professional selects to record Pharmacy First Minor Illness Consultation Information for a Patient

Then the Pharmacy First Minor Illness Consultation Information is recorded

Acceptance criterion 2: record Pharmacy First Urgent Medicine Supply Consultation Information for Patients

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Consultation Information for Patients

When the Health or Care Professional selects to record Pharmacy First Urgent Medicine Supply Consultation Information for a Patient

Then the Pharmacy First Urgent Medicine Supply Consultation Information is recorded

Acceptance criterion 3: record Pharmacy First Clinical Pathways Consultation Information for Patients

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Consultation Information for Patients

When the Health or Care Professional selects to record Pharmacy First Clinical Pathways Consultation Information for a Patient

And the recorded age of the Patient is within the Age criteria for the Clinical Pathway

And the recorded sex of the Patient meets the Sex at Birth criteria for the Clinical Pathway

And the quantity of medication recorded does not exceed the Quantity to be Supplied criteria in the Patient Group Direction (PGD) for the Clinical Pathway

Then the Pharmacy First Clinical Pathway Consultation Information is recorded

Acceptance criterion 4: view Pharmacy First Consultation Information for Patients

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Consultation Information for Patients

When the Health or Care Professional selects to view Pharmacy First Consultation Information for a Patient

Then the Consultation Information is displayed

Acceptance criterion 5: export Pharmacy First Consultation Information for Patients

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Consultation Information for Patients

When the Health or Care Professional selects to export Pharmacy First Consultation Information for a Patient

Then the Consultation Information is exported as a PDF

E00625 - Additional Implementation Details

Solutions MUST comply with the following when implementing this Epic:

  • Consultation Information to be recorded and held in accordance with Records Management Code of Practice

  • Consultation Information to align to the data recording principles in the Advanced Service Specification for Pharmacy First

  • Support Consultation Information recording for Patients with or without a recorded NHS Number

  • Pharmacy First Clinical Pathways Consultation Information to adhere to

    • Pharmacy First Clinical Pathways for Age criteria and Sex at Birth criteria

    • Pharmacy First Patient Group Directions (PGD) for Quantity to be Supplied

  • For Pharmacy First Urgent Medicine Supply Consultation Information the Quantity Supplied of Schedule 3, 4 and 5 Controlled Drugs listed in Pharmacy First Urgent Medicine Supply Schedule 3, 4 and 5 Controlled Drugs 5 Days Maximum Quantity Data and any related Actual Medicinal Products (AMPs) or Actual Medicinal Product Packs (AMPPs), to not exceed the Maximum Quantity

  • Prevent the recording of the following Controlled Drugs using the Pharmacy First Urgent Medicine Supply Service, as their pack size exceeds the Maximum Quantity for 5 days supply:

    • Testogel 16.2mg/g gel (Besins Healthcare (UK) Ltd) 88 gram
      AMPP Code - 35020411000001100

    • Tostran 2% gel (Advanz Pharma Germany GmbH) 60 gram
      AMPP Code - 11558611000001100

    • Tostran 2% gel (Advanz Pharma Germany GmbH) 180 gram 3 x 60g pump
      AMPP Code - 11558711000001100

E00625 - Supporting Information

  • See Data Standards Standard for requirements related to dm+d reference data updates

  • An example message when the recorded Quantity Supplied value exceeds the Maximum Quantity allowed is as follows:
    ”Quantity recorded exceeds Maximum Quantity permitted via this service. Please signpost to an appropriate alternative service if further supply is needed”

  • Best Practice Guidelines for capturing Consultation Information can be found in Community Pharmacy Best Practice Guidelines to Improve Usability in Digital Systems
