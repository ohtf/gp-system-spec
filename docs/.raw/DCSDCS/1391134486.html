Description

Enables Health or Care Professionals to manage Care Plans for a Patient/Service User.

Outcomes

For a Patient/Service User:

Collects information relevant to the effective and coordinated care of Patients with long term conditions or needs into a single location.

For a Health or Care Professional:

Enables the ability to deliver care to a Patient/Service User according to the agreed plan, by providing easy access to a Patient/Service User’s information.

MUST Epics - Describes the minimum functionality required to deliver a Capability. Solutions MUST be successfully evaluated against each Epic and Acceptance Criteria via Capability Assessment in order to be associated with this Capability

E00485 - manage Care Plans

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to manage Care Plans for Patients/Service Users

So that Care Plans are managed

Acceptance criterion 1: create a Care Plan

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Care Plans for Patients/Service Users

When the Health or Care Professional selects to create a Care Plan for a Patient/Service User

Then the Care Plan is created for the Patient/Service User

Acceptance criterion 2: view a Care Plan

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Care Plans for Patients/Service Users

When the Health or Care Professional selects to view a Care Plan

Then the Care Plan for the Patient/Service User is displayed

Acceptance criterion 3: amend a Care Plan

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Care Plans for Patients/Service Users

When the Health or Care Professional selects to amend a Care Plan for a Patient/Service User

Then the Care Plan is amended for the Patient/Service User

Acceptance criterion 4: close a Care Plan

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Care Plans for Patients/Service Users

When the Health or Care Professional selects to close a Care Plan for a Patient/Service User

Then the Care Plan is recorded as closed for the Patient/Service User

E00485 - Supporting Information

  • Examples of Care Plans MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • End of Life Care Plan - contains a Patient/Service User's preferences and wishes for their end of life care

    • Urgent Care Plan - contains a Patient/Service User's preferences and wishes in an urgent care setting

    • Transitional Care Plan - contains anticipated changes in a Patient/Service User's health status, helping them to manage key transition periods in their life and their care trajectory

    • Advanced Care Plan - contains a Patient/Service User’s preferences and wishes regarding their care, to be enacted if they are unable to express a preference in the future

    • Escalation Plan - contains the support the Patient/Service User would receive when managing potential future events relating to their condition(s). It may cover what to do when a Patient/Service User's condition deteriorates and may include instructions for emergency care professionals (e.g. paramedics)
