Description

Enables Citizens to manage their Appointments online. Supports the use of Appointment Slots that have been configured in Appointments Management - GP.

Outcomes

For a Patient:

The ability to view, book and cancel Appointments online efficiently, in a manner that suits their needs and with easy visibility of future booked Appointments and available Appointment Slots.

For Primary Care:

The effective management of time, personnel, and resources to ensure Patients have timely access to appropriate medical services. As a result, there will be fewer phone calls/face-to-face transactions with Patients, releasing time for staff to be deployed on other tasks.

MUST Epics - Describes the minimum functionality required to deliver a Capability. Solutions MUST be successfully evaluated against each Epic and Acceptance Criteria via Capability Assessment in order to be associated with this Capability

E00305 - Patient managing Appointments

As a Patient

I want to manage my Appointments using Citizen Services

So that I can manage my Appointments without contacting the Healthcare Organisation

Acceptance criterion 1: book Appointments

Given the Patient is enabled for the Appointments Management Citizen Service

When the Patient selects to book an Appointment

Then the Appointment is booked

Acceptance criterion 2: view an Appointment

Given the Patient is enabled for the Appointments Management Citizen Service

When the Patient selects to view an Appointment

Then the Appointment is displayed

Acceptance criterion 3: cancel Appointments

Given the Patient is enabled for the Appointments Management Citizen Service

When the Patient selects to cancel an Appointment

Then the Appointment is cancelled 

E00305 - Additional Implementation Details

Solutions MUST comply with the following when implementing this Epic:

  • Utilise Provider IM1 Patient interface(s) and adhere to IM1 - Interface Mechanism Standard (Patient interface Consumer requirements)

  • Patients can only book available Appointment Slots as defined by the Healthcare Organisation

E00305 - Supporting Information

  • For further information on enabling the Appointments Management Citizen Service see manage Appointments Management Citizen Service access for a Patient and the Practice in the Patient Information Maintenance - GP Capability

  • For further information on available Appointment Slots see manage Appointment Slots within a Session in the Appointments Management - GP Capability

  • Further information on Citizen Services can be found in the Citizen Services Implementation Guidance
