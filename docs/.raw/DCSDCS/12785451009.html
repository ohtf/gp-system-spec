Community Pharmacy Clinical Services

Description

Supports the management of Inbound Referrals into Community Pharmacy from other Health or Care Organisations.

Outcomes

For Community Pharmacy Clinical Services:

Management of Inbound Referrals with technology provides better efficiency to Health or Care Organisations by using mechanisms to manage Referrals.

For a Patient:

Requests to relevant Health and Care Organisations for advice, treatment or handover of care that is efficiently managed, in turn providing appropriate care for the Patient.

For Health or Care Organisations:

Reduces the dependency on GP Practices, where clinically appropriate, by allowing Health and Care Organisations to send Referrals to Community Pharmacies.

E00639 - view Community Pharmacy Inbound Referrals from NHS 111 for Patients

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to view Community Pharmacy Inbound Referrals from NHS 111 for Patients

So that I can review Inbound Referrals from NHS 111 for Patients

Acceptance criterion 1: view Community Pharmacy Inbound Referrals from NHS 111 for Patients

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Inbound Referrals from NHS 111 for Patients

When the Health or Care Professional selects to view an Inbound Referral from NHS 111

Then the NHS 111 Inbound Referral is displayed

E00639 - Additional Implementation Details

Solutions MUST comply with the following when implementing this Epic:

  • 111 Standard for the following services:

    • Pharmacy First Minor Illness

    • Pharmacy First Clinical Pathways

    • Pharmacy First Urgent Medicine Supply

E00641 - manually create Community Pharmacy Inbound Referrals for Patients

Acceptance criterion 1: manually create Community Pharmacy Inbound Referrals for Patients

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Inbound Referrals for Patients

    • Self Referral

    • Referrals received by NHS.net Connect (formerly NHSmail)
