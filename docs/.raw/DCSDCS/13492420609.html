Contracting Vehicle(s)

Community Pharmacy Clinical Services

Description

Enables Health or Care Professionals to record Community Pharmacy Consultation Information for Patients and submit this Consultation Information to the Patient’s registered GP Practice and the NHSBSA.

Outcomes

For a Patient:

There is a detailed and standardised history of consultations recorded and submitted to their registered GP Practice.

For Community Pharmacy Clinical Services:

Management of Consultations with technology provides better efficiency to the Organisation by using mechanisms to record and inform other Health or Care Organisations.

E00624 - record Patient Consent to share Consultation Information for Community Pharmacy Contraception Consultations

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to record Patient Consent for sharing Community Pharmacy Contraception Consultation Information with the Patient’s registered GP Practice

So that the Patient’s Consultation Information can be shared with the Patient’s registered GP Practice

Acceptance criterion 1: record Patient Consent to share Consultation Information for Community Pharmacy Contraception Consultations

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Consultation Information for Patients

When the Health or Care Professional selects to record Patient Consent to share Consultation Information for Contraception Consultations

Then the Patient Consent preference is recorded

E00625 - manage Community Pharmacy Consultation Information for Patients

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to manage Community Pharmacy Consultation Information for Patients

So that a full history of Consultations can be maintained for each Patient

Acceptance criterion 1: record Consultation Information for Patients

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Consultation Information for Patients

When the Health or Care Professional selects to record Consultation Information for a Patient

Then the Consultation Information is recorded

Acceptance criterion 2: view Consultation Information for Patients

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Consultation Information for Patients

When the Health or Care Professional selects to view Consultation Information for a Patient

Then the Consultation Information is displayed

Acceptance criterion 3: export Consultation Information for Patients

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Consultation Information for Patients

When the Health or Care Professional selects to export Consultation Information for a Patient

Then the Consultation Information is exported as a PDF

E00625 - Additional Implementation Details

Solutions MUST comply with the following when implementing this Epic:

  • Consultation Information to be recorded and held in accordance with:

    • Records Management Code of Practice

  • Consultation Information to align to the data recording principles for the following services:

    • Pharmacy First

    • Pharmacy Contraception

    • Pharmacy Blood Pressure Check

Acceptance criterion 1: notify Community Pharmacy Health or Care Professional to signpost Patients to their registered GP Practice

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Consultation Information for Patients

When the Health or Care Professional selects to record a Community Pharmacy Consultation for a Patient

Then the Health or Care Professional is notified to signpost the Patient to their registered GP Practice

E00630 - Supporting Information

  • Examples of when a Health or Care Professional is notified to signpost the Patient to their registered GP Practice MAY include, but is not limited to:

    • Guidance on Pharmacy Blood Pressure Check

    • As a result of following NICE Clinical Knowledge Summaries (CKS) guidelines

    • The Patient has presented with a condition that the Health or Care Professional cannot help with and requires further support from their registered GP Practice
