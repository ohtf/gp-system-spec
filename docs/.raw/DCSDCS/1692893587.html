Full or Partial Capability status. For this Capability, Solutions are required to meet a minimum of one MUST EPIC and associated acceptance criteria but not all MUST EPICs (where there are multiple MUST EPICS) to achieve Partial Capability Status, or; to meet all MUST EPICs and their associated acceptance criteria to achieve Full Capability Status.

Description

Enables Citizens and Patients that use health monitoring Solutions to share monitoring data with health and care professionals to support remote delivery of care and increase self-care outside of clinical settings.


Outcomes

For a Patient: Using medical devices and monitoring equipment at home or on the move to regularly record and share vital health information with clinicians to receive support and assistance.
For General Practice:Real time remote monitoring of patients to assist in diagnosis, support and decision making for prevention and anticipatory care.
For the wider NHS and social care system:Supporting and facilitating the provision of health and social care in non-clinical settings to best support the needs of all Patients regardless of their location.


MUST Epics - Epics and acceptance criteria will be evaluated during the Capability Assessment Stage of Onboarding

Epic 1 - share monitoring data with my General Practice

As a Citizen

I want to share my monitoring data with my General Practice

So that my monitoring data are shared with my General Practice

Acceptance criterion 1: share monitoring data with my General Practice

Given the Citizen consents to share monitoring data with their General Practice

When the Citizen chooses to share monitoring data with their General Practice

Then the monitoring data are shared with their General Practice


Epic 2 - configure Telehealth for the Practice

As a Practice User

I want to configure the Telehealth service for the Practice

So that the Telehealth service is configured for the Practice

Acceptance criterion 1: enable the Telehealth service 

Given the Practice User is permitted to enable the Telehealth service for the Practice

When they choose to enable the Telehealth service for the Practice

Then the Telehealth service is enabled for the Practice

Acceptance criterion 2: disable the Telehealth service 

Given the Practice User is permitted to disable the Telehealth service for the Practice

When they choose to disable the Telehealth service for the Practice

Then the Telehealth service is disabled for the Practice


Epic 3 - configure Telehealth for the Patient

As a Practice User

I want to configure the Telehealth service for a Patient

So that the Telehealth service is configured for the Patient

Acceptance criterion 1: enable the Telehealth service 

Given the Practice User is permitted to enable the Telehealth service for a Patient

When they choose to enable the Telehealth service for a Patient

Then the Telehealth service is enabled for the Patient

Acceptance criterion 2: disable the Telehealth service

Given the Practice User is permitted to disable the Telehealth service for a Patient

When they choose to disable the Telehealth service for a Patient

Then the Telehealth service is disabled for the Patient


Epic 4 - manage incoming Telehealth data

As a Practice User

I want to manage incoming Telehealth data

So that Telehealth data is managed

Acceptance criterion 1: manage incoming Telehealth data

Given the Telehealth service is enabled by the Practice

When the Telehealth data is received

Then the Telehealth data is managed
