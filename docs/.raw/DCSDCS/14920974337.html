Description

Supports the registration of Patients and the maintenance of all Patient personal information including demographics. Supports the organisation and presentation of a comprehensive Electronic Patient Record (EPR) and enables other organisations to access this information.

Also supports configuring access to Citizen Services and the submission of data to the Authority for monitoring, research and payment purposes.

Outcomes

For a Patient:

The accurate recording of a Patient’s personal information which contributes to the identification of the Patient and sharing of correct data across health and care. Increased involvement in their own care through enablement of Citizen Services. Patient safety is increased and facilitates finding Patients at risk.

For General Practice:

The organisation and presentation of a comprehensive Electronic Patient Record (EPR) ensures up to date and relevant information is available to appropriate staff to support timely Patient care. It supports effective communication with Patients and other organisations as well as forming the basis for enabling access to Citizen services.

For the wider NHS and social care system: 

The accurate recording of a Patient’s personal information, the organisation and presentation of a comprehensive Electronic Patient Record (EPR) and making it available across care settings improves efficiency, promotes collaboration and encourages the use of a lifelong integrated care record.

E00755 - Additional Implementation Details

  • When Prospective Access to Full Record Access Level is enabled as the default for the View Record Citizen Service, for Patients newly enabled for online access (Practice enabled and via NHS Login) the Access Level is to default to Summary Information Record Access Level when the Patient is excluded for Prospective Access by one of the following criteria:

    • Patient has one of the following SNOMED CT codes recorded in their Electronic Patient Record (EPR):

      • ‘1364731000000104 - Enhanced review indicated before granting access to own health record (situation)’ without a later recording of ‘1364751000000106 - Enhanced review not indicated before granting access to own health record (situation)’ OR ‘1290311000000106 - Online access to own health record granted following enhanced health record review (finding)’

      • ‘1290301000000109 - Online access to own health record withheld following enhanced health record review (finding)’ without a later recording of ‘1290311000000106 - Online access to own health record granted following enhanced health record review (finding)’

      • '1290331000000103 - Online access to own health record declined by patient (situation)' without a later recording of ‘1290311000000106 - Online access to own health record granted following enhanced health record review (finding)’

    • Patient is under 16 years old

  • When Prospective Access to Full Record Access Level is enabled as the default for the View Record Citizen Service, and the Patient is excluded for Prospective Access (see exclusion criteria above), on the Patient’s 16th Birthday the Access Level will:

    • Automatically update to Summary Information Record Access Level for Patients with no previous access

    • Remain the same for Patients with existing View Record Citizen Service access

  • For Prospective Access to Full Record Access Level, if the start date for data to be available from is before the date the Patient Registration started for the Patient, then the start date for data to be available from is to be the same as their Patient Registration started date
