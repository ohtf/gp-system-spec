Description

Allows Health or Care Professionals to conduct secure live remote Video Consultations with individual or groups of Patients and/or Patient Representatives ensuring they can receive support relating to healthcare concerns when a Video Consultation is most appropriate.

Outcomes

For a Patient

Patient can receive support relating to their healthcare concerns when a Video Consultation is the most appropriate method for delivering that support.

For Health or Care Professional

Health or Care Professionals can conduct consultations remotely with Patients/Patient Representatives over video ensuring care services can be delivered in a personal way regardless of location.
