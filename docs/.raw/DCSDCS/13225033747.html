Description

Enables Health or Care Professionals to send and view Emergency Alerts that indicate an emergency or request for assistance.

Outcomes

For a Health or Care Professional:

Supports the safety and wellbeing of Health or Care Professionals by sending Emergency Alerts to recipients that can be viewed and appropriate action taken.

For a Patient/Service User:

In the event of a medical emergency, a Health or Care Professional can send Emergency Alerts to call for assistance.

MUST Epics - Describes the minimum functionality required to deliver a Capability. Catalogue Solutions MUST be successfully evaluated against each Epic and Acceptance Criteria via Capability Assessment in order to be associated with this Capability.

E00682 - send an Emergency Alert

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to send an Emergency Alert to indicate an emergency or that assistance is required

So that I can signal an emergency is taking place or assistance is required

Acceptance criterion 1: send an Emergency Alert

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to send Emergency Alerts

When the Heath or Care Professional selects to send an Emergency Alert

Then the Emergency Alert is sent

And all specified recipients are notified

E00682 - Additional Implementation Details

Solutions MUST comply with the following when implementing this Epic:

  • The triggering of an Emergency Alert will not be obvious to the subject of the Alert

  • The Emergency Alert can be sent to multiple recipients

E00682 - Supporting Information

  • Devices capable of sending Emergency Alerts MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • A computer desktop or laptop

    • A standalone device (e.g. physical button)

    • A handheld or wearable device

    • A mobile phone

  • Devices MAY send Emergency Alerts without requiring Internet availability, GP Practice Solution availability, or for a Health or Care Professional to be logged in to use

  • Examples of recipients MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • Health or Care Professionals

    • External services (e.g. Emergency services)

E00683 - view and acknowledge an Emergency Alert

As a recipient

I want to view an Emergency Alert that indicates an emergency or that assistance is required

So that appropriate action to resolve the Emergency Alert can be taken

Acceptance criterion 1: view an Emergency Alert

Given the recipient is permitted to view Emergency Alerts

When an Emergency Alert is triggered

Then the Emergency Alert is displayed to all recipients

Acceptance Criterion 2: acknowledge an Emergency Alert

Given the recipient is permitted to acknowledge Emergency Alerts

When a recipient selects to acknowledge an Emergency Alert

Then the Emergency Alert is acknowledged

And all recipients are notified of the acknowledgement

E00683 - Additional Implementation Details

Solutions MUST comply with the following when implementing this Epic:

  • The Emergency Alert to include the location and name of the triggering Health or Care Professional

  • If covering multiple locations, the Emergency Alert to also include the Health or Care Organisation location details

  • The notification of acknowledgement of an Emergency Alert will not be obvious to the subject of the Alert

E00683 - Supporting Information

  • Health or Care Professionals MAY view or acknowledge an Emergency Alert without being logged in

  • Emergency Alerts MAY support multiple acknowledgements or remain visible after acknowledgement

E00684 - test Emergency Alerts

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to test Emergency Alerts

So that I know that the Emergency Alerts for my Health or Care Organisation work as expected

Acceptance criterion 1: test Emergency Alerts

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Emergency Alerts

When the Health or Care Professional selects to test an Emergency Alert

Then the Emergency Alert is tested

And the recipients are notified that it is a test

E00684 - Supporting Information

  • Examples of tests for Emergency Alerts MAY include but are not limited to:

    • Test to ensure that all recipients receive a notification

    • Test to ensure that all recipients can acknowledge a notification

    • Test different types of Alert - i.e. Evacuation

    • Test printing or sharing an evacuation list

    • Where a Health or Care Organisation has multiple locations, each location’s Alerts can be tested individually
