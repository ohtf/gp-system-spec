Description

Enables the creation of Service Schedules based on the requirements defined for services and on the resources available across a collection of Health or Care Organisations.

Outcomes

For a Patient/Service User:

Maximises the availability of resources to best meet the health or care needs of the population.

For a Health or Care Professional:

Able to centrally manage the availability, scheduling and allocation of service resources (e.g. staff, equipment, rooms) across a number of organisations.

For a Health or Care Organisation:

Gain efficiencies across a number of organisations by minimising the number of empty rooms across the organisations, minimising travel for staff and maximising the availability or use of specific equipment.

E00514 - manage Service Schedules

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to manage Service Schedules

So that Service Schedules can be defined for Health or Care Organisations

Acceptance criterion 1: create Service Schedule

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Service Schedules

When the Health or Care Professional selects to create a Service Schedule

Then the Service Schedule is created

And any unmet requirements of the service are identified

Acceptance criterion 2: view Service Schedule

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Service Schedules

When the Health or Care Professional selects to view a Service Schedule

Then the Service Schedule is displayed

Acceptance criterion 3: amend a Service Schedule

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Service Schedules

When the Health or Care Professional selects to amend a Service Schedule

Then the Service Schedule is amended

Acceptance criterion 4: delete Service Schedule

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Service Schedules

When the Health or Care Professional selects to delete a Service Schedule

Then the Service Schedule is deleted

E00514 - Supporting Information

  • Examples of requirements defined for a service MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • Staff roles

    • Specific locations

    • Specific equipment
