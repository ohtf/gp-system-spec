Description

Supports the effective and safe prescribing of medicinal products and appliances to Patients. Information relating to prescribable items will be available at the point of prescribing.

Outcomes

For a Patient:

Timely access to the right medicinal products and appliances.

For Primary Care:

Supporting the prescription of medicinal products and appliances that are appropriate and safe for the individual Patient and improve Patient safety through access to information on the risk of potential adverse effects of medication.

For the wider NHS and social care system:

Efficient and effective use of resources to improve health outcomes for Patients through the appropriate selection and provision of medicinal products and appliances.

E00092 - access pre-configured prescribable items and related information at the point of prescribing

As a Health or Care Professional 

I want to access pre-configured prescribable items and related information when creating a medication to prescribe to a Patient

So that pre-configured prescribable items can be selected to create the medication for Patients

Acceptance criterion 1: access prescribable items when creating Patient medication

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage prescribed medication

When the Health or Care Professional selects to create a medication for a Patient

Then they can select from pre-configured prescribable items

Acceptance criterion 2: view prescribable item information when creating Patient medication

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage prescribed medication

And they have selected to create a medication for a Patient

When the Health or Care Professional selects to view pre-configured information for a prescribable item

Then Significant side-effects are displayed

And Cautions are displayed

And Indications are displayed

And Contraindications are displayed

And Dosage Information are displayed

And Interactions are displayed

And Active ingredients are displayed

E00092 - Additional Implementation Details

Solutions MUST comply with the following when implementing this Epic:

  • Pre-configured prescribable Items will adhere to SCCI0052 Dictionary of medicines and devices (dm+d)  

  • Pre-configured prescribable items will include all products licensed under the Medicines Act:

    • Prescription Only Medicines (POMs)

    • Pharmacy Medicines (P)

    • General Sales List (GSLs)

    • Advisory Committee on Borderline Substances (ACBS) approved substances

    • Items in part IX and part X of the Drug Tariff

  • For pre-configured prescribable items within the Drug Tariff, the information available will include:

    • NHS price (e.g. pack price, which would necessitate display of pack size)

    • NHS prescribing status and legal category at pack and formulation level

    • Black Triangle status

    • Indicate if the item is listed in Schedule 2 (SLS items - Part XVIIIB of Drug Tariff) and/or Schedule 1 (Blacklist items - Part XVIIIA of the Drug Tariff)

    • Appliances currently listed in Part IX of Drug Tariff and which are prescribable on the NHS
