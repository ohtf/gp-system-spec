Description

Supports the performing of Risk Stratification using pre-defined criteria that can be run against Patient data.

Outcomes

For a Patient:

Patients identified as at risk may receive preventative care.

For Primary Care:

Able to proactively identify at-risk Patients.

MUST Epics - Describes the minimum functionality required to deliver a Capability. Solutions MUST be successfully evaluated against each Epic and Acceptance Criteria via Capability Assessment in order to be associated with this Capability.

E00505 - perform Risk Stratification

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to perform Risk Stratification to identify Patients who are at risk

So that the management of their care can be prioritised

Acceptance criterion 1: perform Risk Stratification

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to perform Risk Stratification

When the Health or Care Professional selects to perform Risk Stratification

Then the risk level is calculated

And the results are displayed

E00505 - Supporting Information

  • An example of a Risk Stratification tool MAY include, but is not limited to:

    • electronic Frailty Index (eFI)

E00506 - view criteria used for Risk Stratification

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to view the criteria used for Risk Stratification

So that I can review the criteria used to calculate the risk level for Patients

Acceptance criterion 1: view criteria used for Risk Stratification

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to perform Risk Stratification

When the Health or Care Professional selects to view the criteria used for Risk Stratification

Then the criteria are displayed
