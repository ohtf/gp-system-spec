Description

Enables reporting and analysis using a range of Online Consultations and Online Patient Request data items to support clinical care and Health or Care Organisation management. Also supports submission of data to the Authority to support understanding of demand and activity within Healthcare Organisations.

Outcomes

For Primary Care:

Have the tools to be able to analyse Online Consultation and Online Patient Request data locally, which will support areas such as clinical care, audit and operational needs.

For the wider NHS and social care system:

Able to use Management Information (MI) about Online Consultations and Online Patient Requests to improve services and access to information.

MUST Epics - Describes the minimum functionality required to deliver a Capability.

E00577 - report on Online Consultations and Online Patient Requests

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to have access to reports on Online Consultations and Online Patient Requests

So that I can make informed decisions

Acceptance criterion 1: report on Online Consultations

Given there is Online Consultation data available

And the Health or Care Professional is permitted to view Online Consultation reports

When the Health or Care Professional selects to view an Online Consultation report

Then the report results are displayed

Acceptance criterion 2: report on Online Patient Requests

Given there is Online Patient Requests data available

And the Health or Care Professional is permitted to view Online Patient Request reports 

When the Health or Care Professional selects to view an Online Patient Requests report

Then the report results are displayed

E00577 - Supporting Information

  • Examples of reports MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • Report on outcomes or dispositions provided to the Patient

    • Report on the status of Online Consultations and Online Patient Requests

    • Report on Patient demographics using the Online Consultation Solution

E00578 - Online Consultations and Online Patient Requests data collection

As a Health or Care Professional

I want Online Consultations and Online Patient Requests data to be submitted to the Authority

So that information can be submitted to the Authority to support understanding of demand and activity within a Healthcare Organisation

Acceptance criterion 1: create Online Consultations and Online Patient Requests data for submission

Given there is Online Consultations and Online Patient Requests data to be submitted by the Solution

When the creation of the Online Consultations and Online Patient Requests data file is triggered

Then the Online Consultations and Online Patient Requests data file is created

E00578 - Additional Implementation Details

Solutions MUST comply with the following when implementing this Epic:

  • Online Consultations and Video Consultations Reporting Standard (Requirement OCVCR1)

E00581 - configure Online Consultations and Online Patient Requests reports

As a Health or Care Professional

So that I can create reports tailored to the needs of the Healthcare Organisation

E00582 - view demand forecast for Online Consultations and Online Patient Requests

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to view demand forecasts for Online Consultations and Online Patient Requests

So that I can understand the demand and activity within my Healthcare Organisation

Acceptance criterion 1: configure demand forecast for Online Consultations and Online Patient Requests

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to configure demand forecasts for Online Consultations and Online Patient Requests

When the Health or Care Professional selects to configure a demand forecast for Online Consultations and Online Patient Requests

Then the demand forecast is configured

Acceptance criterion 2: view demand forecasts for Online Consultations and Online Patient Requests

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to view demand forecasts for Online Consultations and Online Patient Requests

When the Health or Care Professional selects to view a demand forecast for Online Consultations and Online Patient Requests

Then the demand forecast is displayed

Acceptance criterion 3: view data and method used to create demand forecast for Online Consultations and Online Patient Requests

Given a demand forecast for Online Consultations and Online Patient Requests has been created

When the Health or Care Professional selects to view the data and the method used to create the demand forecast for Online Consultations and Online Patient Requests

Then the data and the method used to create the demand forecast is displayed

E00582 - Supporting Information

  • Examples of configuring demand forecasts MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • Select future time period for demand forecasting

    • Select historical data for demand forecasting

  • Online Consultations and Online Patient Requests forecasts MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • Online Consultations volumes

    • Online Patient Requests volumes

    • Estimated Health or Care Professional resources required to meet Online Consultations and Online Patient Requests demand

  • Examples of methods used for demand forecasting MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • The data items used

    • Values used for data items

    • Any calculations, formula or algorithm used
