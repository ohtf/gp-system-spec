# Historical sources

These sources trace the development of GP system requirements. Historical professional reports are not formal certification or procurement requirements.

## Royal College of General Practitioners, 1980

*Computers in Primary Care: Report of the Computer Working Party*, Royal College of General Practitioners, Occasional Paper 13, June 1980. ISBN `0-85084-073-2`.

- [Read the searchable transcript](rcgp-1980/transcript.md)
- [Open or download the original PDF scan](rcgp-1980/computers-in-primary-care.pdf)

Chapter 1 describes desirable attributes of a GP record system. Chapter 3 proposes how practices could introduce a computerised system in stages. Both copies are hosted in this archive. Use the original scan to check the OCR, particularly for figures, layout and exact quotations.
