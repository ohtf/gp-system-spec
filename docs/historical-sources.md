# Historical and informal sources

These sources describe earlier thinking and informal ideas. Neither establishes certification or procurement requirements.

## Royal College of General Practitioners, 1980

*Computers in Primary Care: Report of the Computer Working Party*, Royal College of General Practitioners, Occasional Paper 13, June 1980. ISBN `0-85084-073-2`. See the [bibliographic record and scan at PubMed Central](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2573752/).

Chapter 1 describes desirable attributes of a GP record system. Chapter 3 proposes how practices could introduce a computerised system in stages. See the [archive record and checksum](https://github.com/ohtf/gp-system-spec/tree/main/rcgp-1980).
