# About the archive

The catalogue records sources, jurisdictions and editions for researchers comparing general practice clinical systems over time. It is independent of the source publishers. It does not process patient data or provide a clinical or conformance tool.

## What is published here

This site contains descriptions, capture dates and links to the publishers and to [the GitHub repository](https://github.com/ohtf/gp-system-spec#collections). The mirrored pages, PDFs, attachments and Trello cards are not duplicated on this site. Live links can change after capture. A capture date records when material was retrieved, not when a requirement became effective.

The catalogue text is licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The linked material retains the rights and notices of its publishers.

## Corrections and rights concerns

Report a broken link, incorrect edition or missing attribution via [the catalogue’s issue tracker](https://github.com/ohtf/gp-system-spec/issues). The [copyright and removal notice](https://github.com/ohtf/gp-system-spec#copyright-and-removal-requests) explains how rights holders can request removal. For a sensitive disclosure, follow the [repository security policy](https://github.com/ohtf/gp-system-spec/security/policy) rather than posting the material in a public issue.
