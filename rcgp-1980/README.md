# RCGP: Computers in Primary Care (1980)

[Original scan](computers-in-primary-care.pdf) of *Computers in Primary Care: Report of the Computer Working Party*. Royal College of General Practitioners, Occasional Paper 13, June 1980. ISBN 0-85084-073-2. [Bibliographic record and public scan](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2573752/).

Chapter 1 lists desirable attributes of a general practice record system. Chapter 3 proposes a phased computerised practice system. This is historical professional guidance, not an NHS procurement specification.
