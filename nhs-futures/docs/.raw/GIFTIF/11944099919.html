Welcome to the Tech Innovation Framework Capabilities and Standards site

The Tech Innovation Framework includes those Capabilities and Standards within the existing GP IT Futures Capabilities and Standards model, with the following changes:

Capabilities

  • The Productivity Capability is not applicable for this Framework

  • The Medicines Verification Capability is not applicable for this Framework

Standards

  • The Primary Care Technology Innovation Standard is mandatory for this Framework

  • The National Medicines Verification System Standard is not applicable for this Framework

Click on relevant Capability or Standard below to view further information.  If you are new to this Wiki, please view the Introduction to Capabilities and Standards page.

To view a short description for a Capability or Standard, click 'More'.

Primary Care Technology Innovation

More....

As part of our move to modernise core clinical systems for Primary Care, NHS Digital have been engaging Suppliers around our Modern Technology Standards. These standards and the NHS Architecture Principles lay out the future ways of working and technology we want to see developing to serve the needs of Primary Care and simplify the complexity of healthcare systems provision.
