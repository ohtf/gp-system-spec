Description

Supports the creation of Referral information for Patients and sending referral requests to the e-Referrals Service (e-RS).

Outcomes

For a Patient:

Requests to relevant Health and Care Organisations for advice or handover of care that is efficiently managed, in turn providing better quality of care for the Patient.

For GP Practice:

Management of Referrals with technology to provide better efficiency to the Organisation, by using mechanisms to create Referral information.

MUST Epics - Describes the minimum functionality required to deliver a Capability. Solutions MUST be successfully evaluated against each Epic and Acceptance Criteria via Capability Assessment in order to be associated with this Capability

E00148 - create Referral information for Patients

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to create Referral information for Patients

So that Referral information is created for Patients when they are referred to other Healthcare Organisations

Acceptance Criterion 1: create Referral information for Patients

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to create Referral information for Patients

When the Health or Care Professional selects to create Referral information for a Patient

Then the Recipient Healthcare Organisation is recorded

And the Recipient Health or Care Professional is recorded

And the reason for the Referral is recorded

And the referring Staff Member’s information is recorded

And the date of the Referral is recorded

And the type of Referral is recorded as a coded clinical term

And the Referral information is created for the Patient

E00148 - Supporting Information

  • Other examples of Referral information MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • Unique identifier (this can be the Unique Booking Reference Number (UBRN) used for e-Referrals)

    • Status

    • Priority

    • Specialty

    • Service type (e.g. outpatient, direct access service, day case, inpatient)

  • For coded clinical terms, see Data Standards for requirements

E00149 - e-Referrals Service (e-RS)

As a Health or Care Professional 

I want to send Referral requests to the e-Referrals Service (e-RS) for Patients

So that Referral requests can be made to the e-Referrals Service (e-RS)

Acceptance criterion 1: send Referral requests to the e-Referrals Service (e-RS)

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to send Referral requests to the e-Referrals Service (e-RS)

When the Health or Care Professional selects to send the Referral request for a Patient

Then the Referral request is sent to the e-Referrals Service (e-RS)
