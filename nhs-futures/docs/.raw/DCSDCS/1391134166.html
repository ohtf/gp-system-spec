Description

Supports the secure management of all forms of unstructured electronic documents including those created by scanning paper documents. Also enables processing of documents and matching documents with Patients/Service Users.

Outcomes

For Health or Care Organisation:

Providing functionality that supports the storage of unstructured electronic documents (e.g. .DOCX or .PDF documents) to reduce organisational and administrative overheads associated with document management.

For the wider NHS and social care system:

Enables the use of automated and semi-automated processes for handling documents, increasing data security and reducing the burden of manual administrative processes.

E00409 - manually manage documents

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to manage documents

So that documents are stored electronically

Acceptance criterion 1: add document

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage documents

When the Health or Care Professional selects to manually add a document

Then the document is added

Acceptance criterion 2: view document

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage documents

When the Health or Care Professional selects to view a document

Then the document is displayed

E00409 - Additional Implementation Details

Solutions MUST comply with the following when implementing this Epic:

  • Documents are to be made available in a timely manner and in non-proprietary formats

  • Proprietary formats are to be converted to non-proprietary formats as required

  • The Solution will ensure the integrity of converted documents and preserve any additional context. Converted documents can therefore be viewed with any original mark ups and annotations intact

E00409 - Supporting Information

  • Examples of manually adding a document MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • Selecting to upload an individual document into the Solution stored locally

    • Selecting to upload all documents from a local folder into the Solution

    • Dragging and dropping a document into the Solution

  • Documents MAY be stored in a document library or in a Patient/Service User record

  • Non-proprietary file formats MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • .DOCX

    • .PDF

    • .TIF
