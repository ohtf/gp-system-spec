Description

Enables Health and Care Professionals to create, view and share Medical Reports for Patients.

Outcomes

For a Patient:

The ability for appropriate information from their Electronic Patient Record (EPR) to be shared with relevant 3rd parties.

For a Health or Care Professional:

The ability to create, view and share Medical Reports for Patients.

MUST Epics - Describes the minimum functionality required to deliver a Capability. Solutions MUST be successfully evaluated against each Epic and Acceptance Criteria via Capability Assessment in order to be associated with this Capability

E00690 - create Medical Reports for Patients

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to create Medical Reports for Patients

So that information from a Patient’s Electronic Patient Record (EPR) can be provided to fulfil a request for a Medical Report

Acceptance criterion 1: create Medical Reports for Patients

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Medical Reports for Patients

When the Health or Care Professional selects to create a Medical Report for a Patient

Then the Medical Report for the Patient is created

Acceptance criterion 2: view content of Medical Reports created for Patients

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Medical Reports for Patients

When the Health or Care Professional selects to view a created Medical Report for a Patient

Then the content of the Medical Report for the Patient is displayed

E00690 - Additional Implementation Details

Solutions MUST comply with the following when implementing this Epic:

  • Solutions which integrate with the GP Core Clinical Solution to adhere to one of the following:

    • GP Connect Access Record HTML Standard (Consumer requirements)

    • GP Connect Access Record Structured Standard (Consumer requirements)

E00690 - Supporting Information

  • For further information on Medical Reports for Patients, see Access to Medical Reports Act 1988

  • Examples of types of Medical Reports MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • Subject Access Requests (SAR)

    • Insurance Reports

    • Employment and Support Allowance or Universal Credit claim reports (See ESA 113 Forms)

    • “Fitness to fly” - Medical Information Form (MEDIF)

    • Medical examination report for a lorry or bus driving licence

E00691 - share Medical Reports for Patients

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to share Medical Reports for Patients

So that information from a Patient’s Electronic Record (EPR) can be shared

Acceptance criterion 1: share Medical Reports for Patients

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Medical Reports for Patients

When the Health or Care Professional selects to share a Medical Report for a Patient

And Patient consent to share the information has been recorded

Then the Medical Report for the Patient is shared

And the shared Medical Report can be attached to the Patient’s Electronic Patient Record (EPR)

E00691 - Supporting Information

  • Examples of methods of sharing Medical Reports MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • Export/Import

    • Electronically (e.g. Email or GP Connect Send Document)

  • Examples of recipients of Medical Reports MAY include, but are not limited to

    • Health or Care Professionals

    • Non-Health or Care Professionals

Acceptance criterion 1: configure what information is automatically included in Medical Reports for Patients

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to configure what information is automatically included in Medical Reports for Patients

E00698 - configure what information is automatically suggested to be redacted in Medical Reports for Patients

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to configure what information is automatically suggested to be redacted in Medical Reports for Patients

So that information that I wish to redact can be identified

Acceptance criterion 1: configure what information is automatically suggested to be redacted in Medical Reports for Patients

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to configure what information is automatically suggested to be redacted in Medical Reports for Patients

When the Health or Care Professional selects to configure what information is automatically suggested to be redacted in Medical Reports for Patients

Then the information that is automatically suggested to be redacted in Medical Reports for Patients is configured

E00698 - Supporting Information

  • Examples of information that could be automatically suggested to be redacted from Medical Reports for Patients MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • Sensitive, 3rd party or potentially harmful material

    • Elements of the Electronic Patient Record (EPR) recorded as Restricted from View Record Citizen Service. For more information, see configure access to elements of the Electronic Patient Record (EPR) in the View Record Citizen Service in the Patient Information Maintenance - GP Capability

    • Firearms licences

E00699 - manage automatically suggested information to be redacted in Medical Reports for Patients

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to accept or reject automatically suggested information to be redacted in Medical Reports for Patients

So that I can redact information from Medical Reports for Patients

Acceptance criterion 1: automatically suggest information to be redacted in Medical Reports for Patients

Given information to be redacted in Medical Reports for Patients has been automatically suggested

When a Medical Report for a Patient is created

Then suggested information to be redacted in the Medical Report for the Patient is displayed

Acceptance criterion 2: approve suggested information to be redacted in Medical Reports for Patients

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to create Medical Reports for Patients

And information has been automatically suggested for redaction

When the Health or Care Professional selects to approve automatically suggested information to be redacted in a Medical Report for a Patient

Then the suggested information is redacted

And the reason for redaction is recorded

Acceptance criterion 3: reject suggested information to be redacted in Medical Reports for Patients

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to create Medical Reports for Patients

And information has been automatically suggested for redaction

When the Health or Care Professional selects to reject automatically suggested information to be redacted in a Medical Report for a Patient

Then the suggested information is not redacted

E00699 - Supporting Information

  • For further information on redaction guidelines, see NHS England - Redaction
