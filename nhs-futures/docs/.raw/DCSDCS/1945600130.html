Appointment Management 

C5E1

GP-06.1-01

Session management - Create, amend, delete Session templates

Create, amend, copy and delete Session templates, each having pertinent information recorded including;

  • a unique identifier or name
  • type (e.g. routine surgery, ante-natal clinic)
  • start time and end time OR Session duration (one can derive the other)
  • Slot Type
  • Slot details (see GP-AP-4.2-01-1 to GP-AP-4.2-01-16)

GP-APMAN-05

Session management - Assign Session templates

Assign Session templates to create a Session for

  • an individual day(s) e.g. Tuesday 3rd July
  • specified days of the week e.g. every day or every other Thursday
  • specific days of the month e.g. first Tuesday of the month or every second Friday of the month
  • a combination of numbers and days e.g. 2nd Thursday of each month
  • other methods including accounting for locally defined closures, e.g. first working day of the month or every Monday except Bank Holidays
  • template start date
  • template end date

GP-06.1-03

Session management - Amend individual Session

Amend or remove an individual Session or any attribute of that Session e.g. remove a Session due to known staff leave or change the staff involved and start time

When a Session's slots are changed and their total duration exceeds the duration of the Session, it is possible to increase the duration of the Session to accommodate the Slot changes. The Session start time, end time or both can change, but the Session will adhere to constraints required by the Practice

See GP-AP-4.1-02A for further detail on rules/restrictions

GP-06.1-04

Session management - Cancel Session

Either

  • Cancel a Session - where active Appointments exist, the Appointment status for each Booking will be automatically updated to 'awaiting re-booking' or automatically re-scheduled and flagged as such
or
  • Prevent cancellation of a Session (until any active Appointments have been cancelled or re-booked in another Session)

C5E3, C5E4

GP-AP-4.1-02A

Session management - Rules/restrictions

Locally define and configure rules/restrictions for Sessions and Slots, including;

  • Restriction or 'blocking out' of individual or multiple Slots within a Session
  • Definition of when Slots can be filled (e.g. open/any time or x hours/days prior to slot time)
  • Prevention of double Booking of resources such as consulting rooms
  • Resources required, including room and equipment combinations
  • Override capabilities - definition of whether the rule/restriction can be overridden. Any rule/restriction to be configured to prevent override, allow override by all, or allow override by specified Practice Users i.e. constrained by user access rights
  • Enabling Sessions and/or individual or multiple Appointment Slots to be made available for Booking online (See GP-SPFS-5.2.2-01A).
    • Where Slots are made available for Booking online, the Practice can configure Slots to be made available for Booking by OSAs 
  • Restricting Sessions and/or individual or multiple Appointment Slots from being opened up to online Booking.

See GP-06.2-05 for requirement on preventing Appointment Bookings and GP-AP-4.2-01-15 on locally defined rules.

See Resource Management (requirements GP-03.4-02 and GP-03.5-02) for how staff details are defined.

GP-AP-4.1-08

Session management - Non-working days

Based on non-working days defined through GP-03.4-07 and GP-RM-1-02, indicate to Practice Users (during recording and when managing a Session) to any Sessions/Appointments that are planned to fall on such a day e.g.:

  • Closed for bank holiday or staff training
  • A specific clinician is on annual leave

A Practice User attempting to book an Appointment on that day/time can see the fact that the resource is not available - locally defined rules/restrictions would govern whether the Appointment can then still be booked despite the lack of resource.

GP-AP-4.4-16

Appointment Management - Non-time bound

Accommodate requests for and manage outcome of non-time bound Appointments - although such Appointments do not have a start time at the point of the request, other dates/times will still be recorded (including the time of request) and the start and/or end time can be recorded retrospectively. Examples of non-time bound Appointments include, but are not limited to:

  • Call-backs (need to telephone a Patient to triage their Appointment)
  • Home visits
  • Unscheduled Appointments (unplanned Appointments where Patients have to sit and wait to be seen or be assigned an order to be seen by e.g. on first come first served basis

GP-06.2-02

Appointment management - Retrieve Appointments

Retrieve available Appointments by browsing/searching/filtering on any combination of the following fields:

  • date/date range
  • day (e.g. Wed or Fri)
  • time (range or period, e.g. am, pm, eve)
  • duration
  • multiple consecutive/adjacent slots
  • session type
  • location
  • slot type
  • slot status
  • staff detail (name, role (grade/level and function/department), languages spoken or gender - any aspect of these characteristics and/or multiple instances of staff to be searchable e.g. Urdu-speaking midwife, a female GP and Health Care Assistant together)
  • booking information
  • locally defined rules/restrictions
  • words within the reason fields (Practice defined and free-text notes)

A specific set of search criteria can be saved with a user-defined name for later re-use by Practice Users.

See GP-AP-4.2-01-6 for detail on Booking information

See GP-AP-4.1-02A for further detail on rules/restrictions
