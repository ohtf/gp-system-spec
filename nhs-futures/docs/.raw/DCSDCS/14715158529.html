Introduction

The GP Connect Emergency Supply of Medications Standard, formerly part of the Digital Medicines (DigiMeds) specification, supports structured emergency medication information to be shared between Healthcare Organisations.

See GP Connect - Emergency Supply of Medications (ITK3) for further information.

Implement and maintain the GP Connect - Emergency Supply of Medications (ITK3) (formerly part of the Digital Medicines (DigiMeds) specification) inline with the latest specification version to send updates of emergency supplies of medication(s) for a Patient at a Community Pharmacy to their registered GP Practice, as per the Data by Capability Mapping section.
