GP-PPFS-6.2-05

Patient Notification of Patient-Practice Communications

Notify a Patient via their preferred Verified Contact Details (see COM1) that they have received a message from the Practice. When the message is related to an existing communication (e.g. a reply to an existing message), only Patients involved in the communication will need to be notified.

MUST
Epic 2

GP-CWP-7

Proxy Notification of Patient-Practice Communications

Notify a Proxy via their preferred Verified Contact Details (see COM1) that they or the Patient have received a message from the Practice. When the message is related to an existing communication (e.g. a reply to an existing message), only Patients and Proxies involved in the communication will need to be notified.

MAY

Practice Notification (Task) of Patient-Practice Communications
