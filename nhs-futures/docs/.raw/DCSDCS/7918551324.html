Vaccinations - Local/PCN Delivery

Description

The PCN Appointments Management - Vaccinations Capability enables the administration and scheduling of vaccination appointments for Patients.

The Capability may also enable:

  • the sending of vaccination invite communications and booking reminders to prompt Patients to book vaccination appointments

  • the sending of vaccination appointment notification and reminders

  • the ability for Patients to manage their own vaccination appointments

Outcomes

Patient:

  • Ability to have vaccination appointments booked, so they can receive vaccinations safely and efficiently as part of the PCN led Vaccination Programme

Healthcare Organisation:

  • Ability to manage vaccination appointment availability for vaccination sites to support the PCN led Vaccination Programme

  • Ability to manage vaccination appointments for Patients registered across different GP Practices

MUST Epics - Epics and acceptance criteria will be evaluated during the Capability Assessment Stage of Onboarding

C46E1 - define appointment availability for a vaccination site

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to manage appointment availability for a vaccination site

So that vaccination appointments can be booked at appropriate times

Acceptance criterion 1: define appointment availability for a vaccination site

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage vaccination appointment availability

When the Health or Care Professional selects to define appointment availability for vaccination appointments for a vaccination site

Then the appointment availability is defined

Acceptance criterion 2: update appointment availability for a vaccination site

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage vaccination appointment availability

And availability for vaccination appointments has been defined for a vaccination site

When the Health or Care Professional selects to update the availability for vaccination appointments for that vaccination site

Then the appointment availability is updated

C46E2 - book vaccination appointments for eligible Patients registered across different GP Practices

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to book vaccination appointments for eligible Patients registered across different GP Practices

So that vaccination appointments can be booked for eligible Patients regardless of which GP Practice they are registered with

Acceptance criterion 1: book vaccination appointments for Patients registered across different GP Practices

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage appointments

And availability for vaccinations appointments has been defined for a vaccination site

And there are eligible Patients registered across different GP Practices who require a vaccination appointment

When the Health or Care Professional selects to book a vaccination appointment for a Patient

Then the appointment is booked

And the appointment is categorised based on the type of vaccination

C46E3 - record that a vaccination appointment for a Patient has been completed

Acceptance criterion 1: record that vaccination appointments for Patients have been completed

When the Health or Care Professional selects to record that a vaccination appointment for a Patient has been completed

Then the appointment is recorded as completed

C46E4 - extract vaccination appointments data for NHS Digital

As a Health or Care Professional

I want vaccination appointments data for Patients to be extracted

So that vaccination appointments data is extracted and can be delivered to NHS Digital

Acceptance criterion 1: extract vaccination appointments data for NHS Digital

Given there is vaccination appointments data for Patients

When the vaccination appointments data extraction is triggered

Then vaccination appointments data is extracted

And the number of booked vaccination appointments for each type of vaccination at a vaccination site is extracted

And the number of completed vaccination appointments for each type of vaccination at a vaccination site is extracted


Supporting Information

  • This requirement can be met via General Practice Appointments Data Reporting or an alternative approach providing it would not result in appointments being duplicated across submissions to NHS Digital
