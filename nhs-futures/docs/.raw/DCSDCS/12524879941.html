Description

Enables Patients/Patient Representatives to request and receive support relating to healthcare concerns online.

Outcomes

For a Patient:

Able to have their consultations conducted online where appropriate, enabling appropriate prioritisation and direction to the most appropriate care.

For a Patient Representative:

Able to have a consultation conducted online on behalf of a Patient where appropriate, enabling appropriate prioritisation and direction to the most appropriate care.

For Primary Care:

Able to assign priority and most appropriate care to Online Patient Consultations through information provided by them, reducing the need for Patients to attend the Healthcare Organisation in person or be available at specific times.

For the wider NHS and social care system:

Improve Patient access to advice or consultation by providing an additional mode of access alongside traditional methods such as the telephone and walk ins.

E00001 - Online Consultation request

As a Patient

I want to create an Online Consultation request

So that I can submit an Online Consultation request to a Healthcare Organisation

Acceptance criterion 1: create an Online Consultation request

Given the Patient has accessed the Online Consultation Solution

When the Patient selects to create an Online Consultation request with the Healthcare Organisation

Then the responses to the Online Consultation request question set are recorded

And the Online Consultation request is created

E00001 - Additional Implementation Details

Solutions MUST comply with the following when implementing this Epic:

  • Patient to be provided with the minimum mandatory Online Consultation request questions based on suggested wording below:

    • What problem do you need help with?

    • What symptoms do you have?

    • How long have you had this?

    • Have you tried to alleviate the problem? 

    • Have you seen anyone about this? 

    • Is there anything you want to tell us? Any ideas, worries or things you are expecting?

    • Preferred contact method?

      • What is the best way to contact you?

        • NHS App

        • Phone

        • SMS

        • Email

    • When are you available?

  • Online Consultation request to include the following demographic and contact details for the Patient:

    • Name

    • Date of birth

    • Sex

    • Address

    • Mobile/Telephone number (where available)

    • Email (where available)

    • NHS Number

E00787 - verify a Patient's demographic details recorded by a Patient Representative using the Personal Demographics Service (PDS) for an Online Consultation request

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to verify a Patient’s demographic details recorded by a Patient Representative are correct using the Personal Demographics Service (PDS)

So that the Patient’s demographic details are accurate

Acceptance Criterion 1: verify a Patient's demographic details recorded by a Patient Representative using the Personal Demographics Service (PDS) for an Online Consultation request

Given the Patient Representative has accessed the Online Consultation Solution

When the Patient Representative selects to create an Online Consultation request on behalf of a Patient

And the Patient Representative has recorded the Patient’s demographic details

Then the Patient’s demographic details are verified using the Personal Demographics Service (PDS)

E00787 - Additional Implementation Details

Solutions MUST comply with the following when implementing this Epic:

  • Personal Demographics Service (PDS) Standard

  • The results from the Personal Demographics Service (PDS) must NOT be shared with the Patient or Patient Representative

  • Where a difference in contact details between those supplied on the Online Patient Consultation request, and those held on Personal Demographics Service (PDS), an indication is to be provided to Health or Care Professionals when viewing the Online Patient Consultation request

E00787 - Supporting Information

  • Verifying the Patient's demographic details supports Health of Care Professionals to identify Patients that are registered with the Practice from those that potentially might not be

  • Where a Patient's demographic details cannot be verified, Health of Care Professionals may wish to utilise functionality such as that described in E00783 - manage unmatched Online Consultation requests

E00542 - include electronic files in an Online Consultation request

As a Patient/Patient Representative

I want to include electronic files in an Online Consultation request

So that I can record additional information to support the Online Consultation request

Acceptance criterion 1: include electronic files in an Online Consultation request

Given the Patient/Patient Representative has selected to create an Online Consultation request

When the Patient/Patient Representative selects to include an electronic file in the Online Consultation request

Then a warning message is displayed

And a description of the electronic file is recorded

And the electronic file is included in the Online Consultation request

Acceptance criterion 2: view electronic files in an Online Consultation request

Given a Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage an Online Consultation request

When the Health or Care Professional selects to view an electronic file in the Online Consultation request

Then the Health or Care Professional can view the electronic file

E00542 - Additional Implementation Details

Solutions MUST comply with the following when implementing this Epic:

  • Warning message displayed to Patient/Patient Representative when including electronic files that contain images to cover:

    • Do not send an intimate image unless this has been agreed with a Health or Care Professional

    • By uploading the image the Patient/Patient Representative consents to providing an image to the Health or Care Organisation

    • Confirmation of who will see any uploaded images e.g. other Health or Care Professionals involved in the Patient/Patient Representative direct care (this may include administrative staff, explaining they are bound by the same duty of confidentiality as clinicians)

    • Confirmation any uploaded image will be used for direct care purposes only

    • Confirmation whether the file and associated image will be stored and why, if stored how and where it will be stored and how long it will be stored for

    • Warning they should not share an intimate image if they do not feel comfortable or feel under pressure

  • Documents are to be made available in a timely manner and in non-proprietary formats

  • If the Solution accepts proprietary formats, they are to be converted to non-proprietary formats as required

  • The Solution will ensure the integrity of converted documents and preserve any additional context

E00542 - Supporting Information

  • Examples of file formats MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • .JPEG

    • .PNG

    • .DOCX

    • .PDF

    • .MP4

E00543 - respond to Online Consultation request from a Patient/Patient Representative

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to respond to an Online Consultation request created by a Patient/Patient Representative

So that I can respond to the Online Consultation request

Acceptance criterion 1: view Online Consultation request

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to view Online Consultation requests

When the Health or Care Professional selects to view an Online Consultation request

Then the Online Consultation request is displayed

And all information recorded by the Patient/Patient Representative for the Online Consultation request is displayed

Acceptance criterion 2: respond to an Online Consultation

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to respond to Online Consultation requests

And the Patient’s demographic details have been verified

When the Health or Care Professional selects to create a response to an Online Consultation request

Then the response is created

And the Patient/Patient Representative can view the response

E00543 - Additional Implementation Details

Solutions MUST comply with the following when implementing this Epic:

  • Initial responses to Online Consultation Requests must be linked to the original Online Consultation Request

  • All additional responses and correspondence between a Patient/Patient Representative and a Health or Care Professional must be linked to the original Online Consultation Request

  • All responses and correspondence can be viewed by Patient/Patient Representative and a Health or Care Professional

E00543 - Supporting Information

  • Examples of a response to a Online Consultation request MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • Clinical advice

    • Clinical information

    • Attach an electronic file

    • Signpost to an online health resource

    • Send a health questionnaire

    • A request for more information

    • A request for an electronic file

    • An Appointment

    • An offer of a self book Appointment

    • A Referral

  • For information relating to the use of links and attachments in Patient communications, see GP-IG-18-10 in Information Governance

E00783 - manage unmatched Online Consultation requests

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to manage unmatched Online Consultation requests

So that I can manage unmatched Online Consultation requests from Patient/Patient Representatives

Acceptance criterion 1: enable unmatched Online Consultation requests into the Healthcare Organisation

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to configure unmatched Online Consultation requests

When the Health or Care Professional selects to enable unmatched Online Consultation requests into the Healthcare Organisation

Then unmatched Online Consultation requests are accepted

Acceptance criterion 2: disable unmatched Online Consultation requests into the Healthcare Organisation

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to configure unmatched Online Consultation requests

When the Health or Care Professional selects to disable unmatched Online Consultation requests into the Healthcare Organisation

Then unmatched Online Consultation requests are rejected

And Patient/Patient Representatives are notified that their Online Consultation request has been rejected

Acceptance criterion 3: view unmatched Online Consultation requests

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to view unmatched Online Consultation requests

When the Health or Care Professional selects to view an unmatched Online Consultation request

Then the details of the unmatched Online Consultation request are displayed

Acceptance criterion 4: match an unmatched Online Consultation request to a Patient registered with the Healthcare Organisation

Given a Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage unmatched Online Consultation requests

When the Health or Care Professional selects to match the unmatched Online Consultation request to a Patient registered with the Healthcare Organisation

Then the Online Consultation request is matched to the Patient

Acceptance criterion 5: manually reject unmatched Online Consultation requests

Given a Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage unmatched Online Consultation requests

When the Health or Care Professional selects to reject an unmatched Online Consultation request

Then the unmatched Online Consultation request is rejected

And the Patient/Patient Representative is notified that their Online Consultation request has been rejected

E00783 - Additional Implementation Details

Solutions MUST comply with the following when implementing this Epic:

  • Rejection notifications to NOT include the reason for rejection

E00783 - Supporting Information

  • Examples of unmatched Online Consultation requests MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • Patient is not registered at the Healthcare Organisation

  • Examples of unmatched Online Consultation request notifications MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • Informing the Patient/Patient Representative that their Online Consultation request has not been submitted

    • Direction to other contact channels or services
