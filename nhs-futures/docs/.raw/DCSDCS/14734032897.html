Description

Enables Patients to share health monitoring data with Health or Care Professionals, to support the remote delivery of care, and increase self-care outside of clinical settings.

Outcomes

For a Patient:

Using medical devices and monitoring equipment at home or on the move to regularly record and share vital health information with Health or Care Professionals, and to receive support and assistance.

For Primary Care:

Remote monitoring of Patients to assist in diagnosis, support and decision making for prevention and anticipatory care.

For the wider NHS and social care system:

Supporting and facilitating the provision of health and social care in non-clinical settings.

MUST Epics - Describes the minimum functionality required to deliver a Capability. Solutions MUST be successfully evaluated against each Epic and Acceptance Criteria via Capability Assessment in order to be associated with this Capability

E00493 - share Remote Patient Monitoring data with Health or Care Organisations

As a Patient

I want to share Remote Patient Monitoring data with Health or Care Organisations

So that Remote Patient Monitoring data is shared with Health or Care Organisations

Acceptance criterion 1: share Remote Patient Monitoring data with Health or Care Organisations

Given the Patient is permitted to share Remote Patient Monitoring data with Health or Care Organisations

When the Patient selects to share Remote Patient Monitoring data with a Health or Care Organisation

Then the Remote Patient Monitoring data is shared with the Health or Care Organisation

E00494 - configure Remote Patient Monitoring for Health or Care Organisations

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to configure Remote Patient Monitoring for a Health or Care Organisation

So that Remote Patient Monitoring is configured for the Health or Care Organisation

Acceptance criterion 1: enable Remote Patient Monitoring

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to configure Remote Patient Monitoring for Health or Care Organisations

When the Health or Care Professional selects to enable Remote Patient Monitoring for a Health or Care Organisation

Then Remote Patient Monitoring is enabled

Acceptance criterion 2: disable Remote Patient Monitoring

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to configure Remote Patient Monitoring for Health or Care Organisations

When the Health or Care Professional selects to disable Remote Patient Monitoring for a Health or Care Organisation

Then Remote Patient Monitoring is disabled

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to configure Remote Patient Monitoring for Patients

So that Remote Patient Monitoring is configured for a Patient

Acceptance criterion 1: enable Remote Patient Monitoring

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to configure Remote Patient Monitoring for Patients

When the Health or Care Professional selects to enable Remote Patient Monitoring for a Patient

Then Remote Patient Monitoring is enabled for the Patient

Acceptance criterion 2: disable Remote Patient Monitoring

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to configure Remote Patient Monitoring for Patients

When the Health or Care Professional selects to disable Remote Patient Monitoring for a Patient

Then Remote Patient Monitoring is disabled for the Patient

E00496 - manage incoming Remote Patient Monitoring data

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to manage incoming Remote Patient Monitoring data

So that Remote Patient Monitoring data is managed

Acceptance criterion 1: manage incoming Remote Patient Monitoring data

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Remote Patient Monitoring data

When the Health or Care Professional selects to manage Remote Patient Monitoring data

Then the Remote Patient Monitoring data is managed

E00496 - Supporting Information

  • The management of incoming Remote Patient Monitoring data MAY include, but is not limited to:

    • Organising received Remote Patient Monitoring data

    • Associating existing Remote Patient Monitoring data with newly received Remote Patient Monitoring data

    • Triggering further actions within the Solution
