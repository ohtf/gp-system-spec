Description

Supports the management of Health or Care Organisation site information including management of Staff Members at Health or Care Organisations.

Outcomes

For Health or Care Organisation:

Information about Staff Members working for the Health or Care Organisation is easier to manage.

For the wider NHS and social care system: 

Efficient and appropriate management of Health or Care Organisation Staff Members to improve services and health outcomes for Patient/Service User.

MUST Epics - Describes the minimum functionality required to deliver a Capability. Solutions MUST be successfully evaluated against each Epic and Acceptance Criteria via Capability Assessment in order to be associated with this Capability.

E00153 - manage my Health or Care Organisation site information

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to manage my Health or Care Organisation site information

So that I can ensure my Health or Care Organisation site information is up to date and accurate

Acceptance criterion 1: create Health or Care Organisation site information

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Health or Care Organisation site information

When the Health or Care Professional selects to create Health or Care Organisation site information

Then Health or Care Organisation site information is created

Acceptance criterion 2: view Health or Care Organisation site information

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Health or Care Organisation site information

When the Health or Care Professional selects to view Health or Care Organisation site information

Then Health or Care Organisation site information is displayed

Acceptance criterion 3: amend Health or Care Organisation site information

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Health or Care Organisation site information

When the Health or Care Professional selects to amend Health or Care Organisation site information

Then Health or Care Organisation site information is amended

Acceptance criterion 4: delete Health or Care Organisation site information

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Health or Care Organisation site information

When the Health or Care Professional selects to delete Health or Care Organisation site information

Then Health or Care Organisation site information is deleted

E00153 - Additional Implementation details

Solutions MUST comply with the following when implementing this Epic:

  • Solutions to support the creation of the following data items:

    • Organisation name (legal)

    • Organisation name (business name)

    • ODS code

    • Organisation address

    • Telephone number

    • E-mail address

E00153 - Supporting Information

  • The primary source of reference data for Health or Care Organisations and individuals is the Organisation Data Service (ODS), see Data Standards for requirements

  • A single Health or Care Organisation can operate from multiple sites (e.g. main site and Branch site for GP Practices)

E00154 - manage information about Staff Members at my Health or Care Organisation

As a Health or Care Professional

I want to manage information about Staff Members at my Health or Care Organisation

So that information about Staff Members at my Health or Care Organisation can be managed

Acceptance criterion 1: create Staff Member information

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Staff Members information

When the Health or Care Professional selects to create information for a Staff Member

Then a Name is created

And a Gender is created

And a Role is created

And a Professional code can be recorded

And nationally recognised identification number can be recorded

And the Staff Member information is created

Acceptance criterion 2: view Staff Member information

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Staff Members information

When the Health or Care Professional selects to view information for a Staff Member

Then the Staff Member information is displayed

Acceptance criterion 3: amend Staff Member information

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Staff Members information

When the Health or Care Professional selects to amend information for a Staff Member

Then the Staff Member information is amended

Acceptance criterion 4: delete Staff Member information

Given the Health or Care Professional is permitted to manage Staff Members information

When the Health or Care Professional selects to delete information for a Staff Member

Then the Staff Member information is deleted

E00154 - Additional Implementation Details

Solutions MUST comply with the following when implementing this Epic:

  • Solutions to support recording of the following information:

    • Professional codes and check digit where appropriate

    • Multiple nationally recognised identification numbers and check digit where appropriate

E00154 - Supporting Information

  • Examples of professional codes MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • GMC code

    • NMC code

    • HCPC code

    • RPS code

    • GDC code

    • GPhC code

    • GOC code

    • Pathology ID

  • See NHS Data Model and Dictionary for more information on Professional Registration Body Codes

  • Examples of nationally recognised identification numbers MAY include, but are not limited to:

    • GMP code

    • Prescriber identifier
