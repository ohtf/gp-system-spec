# Description {#PersonalHealthRecord-Description}

Enables Patients/Service Users to manage their Personal Health Record information relating to their health and care.

# Outcomes {#PersonalHealthRecord-Outcomes}

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| For a Patient/Service User: | Ability to add, view and update their Personal Health Record information about their personal health and care. |
