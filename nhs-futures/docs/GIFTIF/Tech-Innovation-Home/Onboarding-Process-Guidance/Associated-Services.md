# Associated Services

# Summary {#AssociatedServices-Summary}

#### Living Documents {#AssociatedServices-LivingDocuments}

Please note that this guidance is a set of living documents and will be regularly updated.

Some processes have yet to be fully defined - these will be added as they are completed.

The processes are under continual review and will be amended as required.

This section provides details on the purpose and process for registering Associated Services with the Tech Innovation Framework Onboarding Process for the Buying Catalogue.

Registering your Associated Service(s) should take place at the same time that you register your Catalogue Solution.

Your Supplier Manager will provide you with a copy of the Associated Services Form which has been pre-populated with information we already hold, including details that you provided during your tender application.

You should check all of the details and correct any which are wrong or outdated, before emailing your completed forms to [buying.catalogue@nhs.net](mailto:buying.catalogue@nhs.net) and copying in your Supplier Manager.

You should inform your Supplier Manager if we have a record of any Associated Services which are not applicable, or if you are adding any further Associated Services.

# What is an Associated Service? {#AssociatedServices-WhatisanAssociatedService?}

As described in the Catalogue Onboarding Process ancillary document,

*“8.1 An Associated Service is a service associated with the implementation or optimisation of a Catalogue Solution. It is optional for a Supplier to offer Associated Services and buyers cannot be required to purchase an Associated Service. Associated Services will be one-off services, for example, training, or set up. The cost of an Associated Service is not included in the Periodic Service Charge, and may be in a form different from Periodic Service Charge (e.g. a unit of order).”*

You do not have to offer Associated Services and Buyers cannot be required to purchase any Associated Services.

For more information, see the Catalogue Onboarding Process ancillary document.

# Outcomes {#AssociatedServices-Outcomes}

At the end of the Associated Service registration activity you will have:

- received and checked the information we have populated in the Associated Service Forms.
- completed blank Associated Service Forms for any Associated Services of which we are not aware, or added them to existing Associated Services Forms.
- returned your Associated Service Forms at the same time as your Solution Registration Forms.

At the end of the Associated Service registration activity we will have:

- pre-populated your Associated Services Form(s) with any information we already have.
- provided you with your Associated Services Form(s).
- a confirmed record of the Associated Services you wish to register.

# Completing Associated Services Registration {#AssociatedServices-CompletingAssociatedServicesRegistration}

## Actions required of you {#AssociatedServices-Actionsrequiredofyou}

1. Read the “Instructions” tab on the Associated Services form.
2. Review/correct and complete the information already added to Associated Services Form(s), provided by your Supplier Manager.
3. Remove the detaiIs of any Associated Services we have recorded which are not applicable.
4. Update existing or complete new Associated Services Forms for any Associated Services of which we are not aware.
5. Return to us all of your completed Associated Services Forms, along with your Solution Registration Form, to [buying.catalogue@nhs.net](mailto:buying.catalogue@nhs.net), copying in your Supplier Manager.

## Actions required of us {#AssociatedServices-Actionsrequiredofus}

1. Populate your Associated Services Form(s) with information we already hold.
2. Share your pre-populated Associated Services Form(s) with you for review and completion.
3. Reviewed your Associated Service(s) details and queried or confirmed them.
